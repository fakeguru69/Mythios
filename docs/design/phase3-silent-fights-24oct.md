# Phase 3, part 6: the silent man who runs (24 Oct 2026, for Fake Guru)

The plan says: keep three fights against a silent man who breaks away and runs, and turn the rest into something else.
Seven fights end that way today:

| Day | Fight | Pays | What it leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1D1 | A man on the road | 0 (Challenge day; the Day 1 welcome) | the rubbing; the first meeting with the silence |
| W1D3 | The man in the bracken | 300g | a watcher post at the rock |
| W1D5 | The runner from Fenmark | 300g | "the same silence", he runs north |
| W2D5 | The one sent up to look | 300g | the strip of coat, the black circle, the cuff thread (the horn button's payoff) |
| W3D1 | The one who came up after you | 300g | nothing new |
| W3D5 | The grey coat on the ridge | 300g | who strikes first follows the road (and now the Relic is on the other road) |
| W4D5 | The man with the boat | 0 (Challenge day) | the boat, and he swims away into the fog |

**Why this is your decision:** five of these are fight-only days that pay 300g. Turning a fight into a Challenge pays
+500g or costs 150g and grows an Attribute; turning it into a plain beat pays nothing. Either way the season's gold
changes, and the numbers are yours (CLAUDE.md §8: do not invent numbers).

**My suggestion (a):** keep W1D1 (the first time), W2D5 (it pays off the horn button) and W3D5 (its road matters).
Change the other four so they stop being the same fight, without changing any gold:

- **W1D3:** he does not run. He is down, and he says nothing, and while you stand over him he looks past you to the
  village smoke, and somebody there lights a second fire. He is still lying there, tied, when you come back.
- **W1D5:** the runner fights, and loses or wins, and is caught by the woman's dog at the edge of the camp: he gets
  away, but leaves his boot, and its sole is packed with the grey mud of the Fenmark lane.
- **W3D1:** the one who came up after you is not a man. It is the dog from the chain, loose now, sent up the hill
  after you: same pay, a different enemy, and it ends as the chained dog did, lying down.
- **W4D5:** the man with the boat speaks, once, to the fog, not to you: a word you will hear again at the Gathering.
  (Only if the Deception Table or the Gathering has a line for it; otherwise he stays silent and keeps his swim.)

**Other options:** (b) turn W1D3, W1D5 and W3D1 into Challenges (the season's gold moves a little and a Daily player gets three
more growths; I would run the season simulator before you decide); (c) leave all seven.

Nothing is changed until you choose.
