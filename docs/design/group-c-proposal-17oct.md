# Group C: making results matter (a proposal for Fake Guru, 17 Oct)

**Decided 17 Oct by Claude (Fake Guru: "merged. you decide the rest") and built:** C1 yes at +1, Week 6 only; C2 yes at +2; C3 yes from Day 5; C4 yes; C5 yes; C6 reworded ("The hardest yet." removed, DC stays 9). Details in the master tracker, 17 Oct.

These are numbers and rules for you to decide, from the gameplay verdict (`docs/design/gameplay-verdict-17oct.md` §4 C). My suggestion comes first in each item.

The game already has a tool for this: a "harder card" (`harderIf`). An earlier result makes a later Challenge harder and adds one line saying why. Today it is used once, when Gathering 5's United Effort is lost and Week 6 Day 1 becomes 2 harder.

## C1. The Week 6 countdown carries its failures
Today Week 6's failures describe new danger ("all of Highwall hears it", "two more men called up", "the lantern looked this way") that never touches a later roll.

**Suggestion:** a failed Challenge on these days makes the next day's card **1 harder**, with one line saying why.

| A fail on | Makes harder | Why (a line I would draft for your approval) |
|---|---|---|
| Day 2 ("all of Highwall hears it") | Day 3's card, +1 | The square's watch has doubled since last night. |
| Day 3 ("two more men called up") | Day 5's card, +1 (Day 4 is a fight) | The extra men are still on the walls. |
| Day 5 ("the lantern looked this way") | Day 6's card, +1 | They are watching the hill now. |

- +1 is about 5 percentage points; +2 would be about 10.
- I'd keep it to +1 and to Week 6 only, where the countdown should tighten.
- Later we could do the same for Week 5.

## C2. Every lost United Effort carries, not only those at Gatherings 3 and 5
**Suggestion:** a lost United Effort at Gathering 1, 2 or 4 makes the next Week's Day 1 card **2 harder**, as Gathering 5's does, with one line each, drafted for your approval.

## C3. Announce the Gathering's Attribute earlier
Today "Tonight calls on Charm" appears only on Day 7. Giving a matching Relic to the friend who will roll is the one real plan in the economy.

**Suggestion:** show it from **Day 5**, on the party page beside the Gathering's time and in the Day 5 notification, so friends can plan their Relics.

## C4. The last light pays more than a first letter
**Suggestion:** a pass at the last light also shows the clue of one lead nobody took tonight. This helps a small party, which can never see all three clues.

## C5. End the fragment race
Today each fragment word goes to whoever solves it first, and a friend may solve only one word on its day. In a party of three or more, somebody often gets nothing that day.

**Suggestion:**
- Each friend solves the day's fragment in their own Chest and earns their own 25g a word.
- The party's map counts a word as solved once any friend has it (unchanged for the map +2).

## C6. Make Week 6 Day 6 "the hardest yet" true
**Suggestion:** Week 6 Day 6's card goes from DC 9 to **DC 10**. Or, if you'd rather keep the numbers, I reword the card.
