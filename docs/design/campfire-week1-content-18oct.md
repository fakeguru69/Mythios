# The Campfire, version 3, Phase 2: Week 1's content (draft, 18 Oct, for Fake Guru)

Fake Guru: "Phase 2." The rules this follows are in `campfire-v3-17oct.md` (§2 the four jobs, §4 clicks, §5 the night, §8 of `campfire-loop-17oct.md`). Nothing here is in the game until you approve it.

**How to read each night**
- **Tonight's question** is what the fire pins and calls at dawn. It must still be open when the fire opens, and the story must answer it somewhere. **Read back** says where, and the line MYTHIOS shows there.
- **Tonight's puzzle** is a smaller question the evidence can settle tonight: when the two pieces that **click** are both on the table, MYTHIOS says what they show together. If nobody completes the pair, the story gives the answer anyway.
- **The pieces** are the three last-light leads, plus the pass line and the [PRESS ON] find where they bear on the night. Each has a **job** (Reveal, Tilt, Turn, Link) and a **payoff**. Notice lines stay as they are; they are pieces too, but a click never depends on one.
- **Garrick** speaks only where shown. Lines marked **NEW** are new text and need your yes, and the Garrick check below.
- Text marked **NEW** is new; everything else is already in the game, word for word.

**The week's climb.** Day 1 asks who wrote the list; Day 2, whose child Meira was writing about; Day 3, who the watcher works for; Day 4, why Tamsin; Day 5, whether this is one man or a way of working; Day 6, the week's own question, the party's case: why are our names on that list? Each answer narrows the next question.
