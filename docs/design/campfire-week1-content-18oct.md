Fake Guru: "Phase 2." The rules this follows are in `campfire-v3-17oct.md` (§2 the four jobs, §4 clicks, §5 the night, §7 Garrick). Nothing here is in the game until you approve it.

An independent check read every line of the first draft against the episode files and found 13 problems, three of them serious: a casebook thread and a Garrick line that pointed too near the secret, and a click that answered its own night's question. All are fixed below. The list is at the end.

**How to read each night**
- **Tonight's question** is what the fire pins and calls at dawn. It is still open when the fire opens, and the story answers it later. **Read back** says where, and MYTHIOS sets the party's call beside that line.
- **Tonight's puzzle** is a smaller question the evidence can settle tonight. When the two pieces that **click** are both on the table, laid by two different friends, MYTHIOS says what they show together. If nobody completes the pair, the next morning MYTHIOS shows the same line, as what the table would have shown. So no puzzle is ever left hanging.
- **The pieces** are the three last-light leads, plus the pass line and the [PRESS ON] find where they bear on the night. Each has a **job** (Reveal, Tilt, Turn, Link) and a **payoff**. Notice lines stay as they are; they are pieces too, but a click never depends on one.
- **Garrick** speaks only where shown. A nudge that names a piece speaks only once that piece is on the table; otherwise its plain version is used. He never investigates and never concludes.
- Text marked **NEW** is new. Everything else is already in the game, word for word.

**The week's climb**

Scored out of 5 for **debate** (would two friends really disagree?) and **payoff** (does the answer land, and soon enough to feel it?).

|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | 2 | 4 | A teaching night: most clues lean one way, and the next morning answers it. It teaches the loop (pin tonight, the story answers tomorrow) with a quick, sure win. That is right for the first night, alone. |

**Does it make sense?** Every click states only what the story backs, every read-back quotes a real line, and no night's question is already answered when its fire opens. Two small fixes made in this pass:
- The leaned bar is not a Turn, since the chapter already says the watcher came through the gate. It is now a Link to Day 3: he stopped to lean the bar neatly, so he knows that gate.
- Day 6's nudge spoke of "that list today", but Day 6 is the map. It now says the list has been in your pocket all week.

**What could be stronger** (not needed now; for after a playtest):
- Day 1 is gentle. A real Turn piece, true but misleading, would make it a debate. Nothing in the chapter supports one without inventing lore, so I left it.
- Clicks mostly confirm what a careful player suspects. Week 2 has richer material (the paper, the bed, the cart) and will have sharper ones.

**Verdict:** it makes sense and holds together. Days 2, 4, 5 and 6 are genuinely interesting. Day 1 is a deliberate warm-up, and Day 3 is solid.
