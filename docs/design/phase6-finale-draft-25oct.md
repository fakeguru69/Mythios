# Phase 6: the finale, drafted for Fake Guru (25 Oct 2026)

Five changes to Gathering 6 and Week 6 Day 6, each shown as it is now and as drafted. **Built 25 Oct** (Fake Guru: "Proceed next"): all five are in the game and live.

Garrick's lines are unchanged in every draft. No numbers change.

---

## 1. The last choice is honest

**Now.** "THE FIFTH IS IN YOUR HANDS." `[GIVE IT TO HIM]` `[HOLD IT BACK]`. Whatever the majority picks, the fifth reaches
him: given, or he closes his hand over yours. HOLD IT BACK promises a choice the story cannot keep.

**Drafted (my pick).** Keep the vote; rename the second button **`[CLOSE YOUR HAND]`**, so the doom is the point. The
branch reads:

> You close your hand on it. He does not argue. The fight has left his palm bleeding. He reaches out and closes his
> hand over yours, round the Stone, and it answers him anyway, through your fingers. You still carried it to him, into
> this room.

The season card's "the fifth `[given / held back]`" becomes "the fifth `[given / kept in your hand]`".

*The other way* (two real outcomes) needs Season 2 to pay it off, so I would leave it for then.

## 2. Why the party carries the fifth in

**Now.** Week 6 never says why you bring the one thing he needs into his hall; a reader can feel the party walked in
blind.

**Drafted.** One passage on Week 6 Day 6, in the wall room, just before Garrick pours the last of his flask:

> Nobody says aloud that you could leave it behind. The torn page in your coat says *the fifth shall end*, and stops,
> and an ending needs the thing that ends it. Whatever happens in that hall tomorrow, the fifth has to be in it.

It is the party's own reasoning from the torn page, which they have held since Week 4, and it is the exact mistake the
finale turns on.

## 3. Fewer quotes at the punch

**Now.** Straight after the fifth answers him, four quotes arrive as four messages (the torn page, the Verse of the
Binding, the Seekers' wall, the warning Meira glossed), then the Blackglass sliver, then the two Verse Matches: about
seven pieces of scripture inside two minutes.

**Drafted.** The four lines the party already owns arrive **together, as one card laid on the stone** ("What you
carried in"), then Garrick's "You read them as one". The Blackglass sliver stays as the one line alone on the screen.
That leaves three pieces of scripture at the punch: the card, the sliver, the Verse.

## 4. A breath before the act

**Now.** "The light lets go of Jo. One heartbeat. One act."

**Drafted.**

> The light lets go of one of you. A single breath. The hall does not notice.
>
> It is Jo.
>
> **Everyone else: you have thirty seconds. Tell Jo what to do.**

## 5. A silent Match, and the last lines

**Now.** Each Verse Match ends "All of you matched it." The last shared lines end "**To be continued.**", and the season
card ends "What you did in that last heartbeat is remembered. Season 2 begins from it."

**Drafted.**

- The two Matches are silent: the right row lights with the initials of those who matched it, and nothing is said.
- The last shared lines end on the linen and the eyes:

> Tamsin's hand. Corin's. Wren's. Joss's. Yours. The same linen. The same careful knot, every time.
>
> Across the ring of light, the king's eyes have not left you.

- "To be continued." goes. The season card keeps "What you did in that last heartbeat is remembered." and drops
  "Season 2 begins from it."
