# Disguised forks: Week 1 Day 1 sample, and the single buttons sorted (draft, 18 Oct)

Fake Guru, 18 Oct: no single action buttons; two choices that lead to the same place, in disguise ("1, 2, 3 okay").
The rule: two options shown together are a choice, and picking one closes the other. Each road reaches the same
place but leaves one detail only that road gets, so friends comparing at the fire find different pieces.

## Week 1 Day 1: the two roads (draft for his approval; canon lines kept word for word, new lines marked NEW)

**LOOK WHERE HE STOOD**
> A scrap of parchment in the leaf-mould, half burned. Its scorched edge flakes at your touch and still smells faintly
> of smoke. It is not writing and it is not a drawing. Somebody laid it over something hard and raised, and rubbed
> charcoal across it until the shape came through.
>
> You turn it to the light and back again. You do not know the shape.
>
> NEW: It lay face down, just where his feet had been, as if it fell from him when he turned for the trees.
>
> NEW: Walking back to the wagon you see the rest of it. Your cargo box, slewed half off the tilted wagon bed. The
> lock is jammed. The scratches round it are fresh, bright in the dull old metal. They are not yours.
>
> He was at this before you ever saw him.

**LOOK AT WHAT HE WAS AT**
> Your cargo box, slewed half off the tilted wagon bed. The lock is jammed. The scratches round it are fresh, bright in
> the dull old metal. They are not yours.
>
> NEW: They are all on the one side, and low, where a man would kneel to work and still keep half an eye on the road.
>
> He was at this before you ever saw him.
>
> NEW: You look back to where he stood. Something pale lies in the leaf-mould there: a scrap of parchment, half
> burned. Its scorched edge flakes at your touch and still smells faintly of smoke. It is not writing and it is not a
> drawing. Somebody laid it over something hard and raised, and rubbed charcoal across it until the shape came through.
>
> You turn it to the light and back again. You do not know the shape.

Each road's own detail: "it fell from him when he turned" (road one); "kneel to work and keep an eye on the road"
(road two). Checked against the day's six Notice lines: neither repeats one. Neither adds a fact the story contradicts
(the rubbing is Meira's; he carried it, as the carrier reading of Day 1 already allows).

## The single buttons, sorted (77 in the season)
- **Questions and talk (13)**, such as "Ask him about the list": become disguised forks, two questions at once; both
  answers still come, in the order the road takes.
- **Taking the day's fragment (about 36)**, such as "Reach in", "Take it": proposed no button; the fragment is found in
  the story and a card says it has gone to your Chest.
- **Laying out the week's pieces on Day 6 (6)**: no button; the map assembles in the story.
- **"Strike the page" in Week 6 (5)**: kept as Week 6's ritual, or no button (his call).
- **Other handling (about 17)**, such as "Brush your hand across it": each sorted day by day, fork where there is a
  natural second way, otherwise no button.
