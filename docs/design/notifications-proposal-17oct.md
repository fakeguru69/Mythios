# Notifications as a feature (a proposal for Fake Guru, 17 Oct)

Fake Guru: "we should make notification a feature for other players to keep track of other people's movement or achievement, and to entice one to enter the day's play or to go back to campfire to interact w others. But yes, not too baggy, but yet interesting enough. Think this through."

Already decided: no daily limit; the Campfire is where friends meet each day; "Mei has come to the fire." is built (`0089`).

## Four rules for every alert
1. **About people, never the story.** An alert names a friend and what they did ("Mei passed today's Challenge"), never what was found: no clue text, no fragment word, no theory reading, no pass line. A friend who has not played loses nothing, and the fire's pin-before-peek rule holds.
2. **One buzz, then quiet updates.** Three kinds, each with its own label on the phone: **your turn**, **the fire**, **the party**. Within two hours, a new alert of the same kind replaces the one on the phone without buzzing again, and says everything so far ("At the fire: Mei and Dev came. Dev pinned a theory."). After two hours the next one buzzes.
3. **Only to the right people.** Never about yourself; fire alerts never to a friend at the fire at that moment; nothing on Week 1 Day 1 (the party has not met); none to a late joiner still on their own Day 1; no failures pushed. Quiet hours 10:00pm to 8:00am: an alert from 5:00am waits until 8:00am, one after 10:00pm is dropped.
4. **Each player chooses** on the Notifications card: **Everything** (default), **Just my turn and the Gathering**, or **Off**.

## Holes checked
- **Spoilers:** no alert carries story content.
- **Pin before peek:** a pin is announced, its reading never.
- **A party of six:** merging and quiet updates keep a busy day to about 4 to 7 buzzes per friend.
- **A friend who drifts away:** the 7pm nudge stops after three missed days in a row, then canon's one "The road has gone on without you"; it starts again once they play.
- **Shame:** failures are never pushed; they stay at the fire, where they help.
- **Help-calls:** never a push (canon).
- **Gold or advantage:** no alert pays or reveals anything a friend could use against another.
- **Unknown:** iPhones may not support the quiet update and could buzz each time. To be tried on a real phone before relying on it; if so, the fire and party kinds fall back to one alert per kind per two hours on iPhone.
