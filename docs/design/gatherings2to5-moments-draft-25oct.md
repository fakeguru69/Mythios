# Gatherings 2 to 5: one heartbeat each (draft for Fake Guru, 25 Oct 2026)

*Renamed in the 25 Oct audit: the moment is now "One breath", and its lines say "One breath before…". "Heartbeat" may not be said before Week 4 Day 6 (CLAUDE.md §6), and Gatherings 1 to 3 come before it. The text below is the draft as approved; the episode files hold the words in play.*

Phase 4, part 1, the rest. Gathering 1's moment is built, and these four were built and loaded live on 25 Oct (Fake Guru: "Yes"). Each follows Gathering 1's model exactly:

- It comes just before the United Effort begins, as its own short part, "One heartbeat" (not when alone).
- One friend is picked at random (not whoever rolled the Approach). The others have thirty seconds to tell them what to
  do; then that friend has twenty seconds to choose. No tap is the third choice.
- The first choice gives that friend the United Effort's first turn. The second leaves a line later in the night. The
  third leaves things as they are. **No numbers change**, and no Garrick line is added.

Canon's names stand in for the real friends, as everywhere: Mei is whoever the light picks.

## Gathering 2: under the mill hill (before THE BREACH)

> Mei has the black book in one hand and a lamp in the other. One heartbeat before the first of their lamps reaches the
> mouth of the tunnel.
>
> **Everyone else: you have thirty seconds. Tell Mei what to do.**

> `[TAKE THE LAMP TO THE PROPS]` see which timber is holding it, before anyone else has to
> `[DOUSE THE LAMPS]` put out every light in the room but one
> `[STAND FAST]`

*Take the lamp to the props:*
> Mei is at the tunnel's mouth before the rest of you have moved, lamp held high, reading the green timber.

*Douse the lamps:*
> Mei pinches out the lamps one after another, until only one is left, cupped in a hand. In the tunnel the coming lights
> falter, as if the dark had surprised them.

*Stand fast:*
> Mei does not move, and nor do any of you. Whatever comes out of that tunnel, it will find you together.

**Later in the night** (after the United Effort, if the lamps were doused):
> You read what comes next by the one lamp that is left, passed from hand to hand.

## Gathering 3: the Kiln (before THE SCREE)

> Mei is at the foot of the glass, nearest to her. One heartbeat before the slope moves again.
>
> **Everyone else: you have thirty seconds. Tell Mei what to do.**

> `[GO UP FIRST]` onto the glass, with the rope's end, before anyone can stop you
> `[CALL HER NAME]` the name on her mother's lips in Ashcombe, loud enough to carry
> `[KEEP STILL]`

*Go up first:*
> Mei is three steps up the glass before anyone can say a word, rope in one fist, blade in the other.

*Call her name:*
> *"Wren."* High on the slope the girl turns her head, and for the first time since the fight began, she stops trying to
> climb away from you.

*Keep still:*
> Mei does not move. Nobody does. The glass settles with a sound like plates being stacked, and goes quiet.

**Later in the night** (when she is down, if her name was called):
> She does not go to the nearest of you. She goes to the one who called her by her name.

## Gathering 4: the drowned church (before THE WATER-GATE)

> Mei is nearest the gate. The water is at his chin. One heartbeat before he has to choose between the breath and the
> bars.
>
> **Everyone else: you have thirty seconds. Tell Mei what to do.**

> `[GO DOWN FOR THE PIN]` under the water, now, for as long as one breath lasts
> `[HOLD HIS HEAD UP]` kneel in the water behind him and keep his face clear
> `[STAY WHERE YOU ARE]`

*Go down for the pin:*
> Mei is under before anyone can say a word. The cold closes over, and the grey water churns where Mei went.

*Hold his head up:*
> Mei kneels in the black water behind him and takes his head in both hands, and lifts. He does not thank anyone. He
> breathes.

*Stay where you are:*
> Mei does not move. The water laps at his chin, and goes back, and comes again.

**Later in the night** (at the board, if his head was held):
> When he answers, he looks first at the one who held his head out of the water.

## Gathering 5: the dead street (before THE ROLL-CALL)

> Mei is kneeling beside the dying watchman. One heartbeat before the voice on the hill calls the next post.
>
> **Everyone else: you have thirty seconds. Tell Mei what to do.**

> `[ANSWER THE FIRST CALL]` in his voice, before anyone else has to
> `[HOLD HIS HAND]` so that he is not alone while you speak for his dead
> `[KEEP SILENT]`

*Answer the first call:*
> Mei draws one breath and answers up the hill, low and flat and unhurried, the way he did. The voice on the hill says
> nothing, and moves on.

*Hold his hand:*
> Mei takes the watchman's hand. It is cold already, and it closes, once.

*Keep silent:*
> Mei says nothing. The voice on the hill waits, and the wind fills the gap.

**Later in the night** (if his hand was held, when the roll-call is answered):
> He does not let go of the hand that held his.
