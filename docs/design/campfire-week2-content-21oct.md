## Day 1 · The mill road

- **Place:** The last farmhouse before the mill. Barn loft.
- **Tone:** Wry, road-worn. Cup Night.
- **Opener:** *"Beds are in the barn tonight. Pick a corner and take it."*

**Tonight's question:** *A dark cart on an open road. Why pass every farm and take the mill boy?*
- **A:** The mill was the only house they had a map to.
- **B:** Corin was the only child they came for.
- **Read-back the next morning:** At Day 2's *"He asked for my boy by name, and knew the night he was born"* —
  > *At the farm [Friend Name] called it: Corin was the only child they came for.*

**Tonight's puzzle:** *How did the cart travel in the dark?*
- **Click pair:** 2-1-1 (The steady horse) + 2-1-3 (Deeper coming down)
- **Click text (said once in chat when both are laid):**  
  > *"The horse never checked in the dark on the way up, and coming down the wheels sank deep under weight. Whoever drove knew the turn, and left with what they came for."*

**Garrick's Nudge:**

**Fragment 2-1 Meaning Question:**

- **Click text:**

**Garrick's Nudge:**

## Day 3 · Upriver

- **Tone:** Wry, grim.

## Day 4 · The shaft in the hillside

- **Place:** Crouched in the wet roots above the shaft.
- **Tone:** **QUIET NIGHT.** No jokes, no light quips. Corin is bound in a circle of lamps below.
- **Opener:** *"Sit down. Keep your voices under the wind."*

**Tonight's question:** *They pulled the ladder, but they didn't run. Why didn't they scatter when we were spotted?*
- **A:** They have too many guards down there to fear four people.
- **B:** Whatever they are doing to the boy cannot be paused for anything.
- **Read-back at Gathering 2 Arrival:** At *"It is not the sound of people in a hurry. It is the sound of people who have done this many times before"* —

**Tonight's puzzle:** *Are they panicked or deliberate?*
- **Click pair:** 2-4-1 (The second lamp) + 2-4-3 (The chant ran on)
- **Click text:**  
  > *"The heads turned at the shout, but the words of the chant never broke, and the second lamp was trimmed without haste. They are not panicking. They are working to an hour."*

**Garrick's Nudge:**
