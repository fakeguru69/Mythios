## Day 2 · The mill

- **Place:** The working mill. The slow splash of the wheel.
- **Tone:** Wry, tense.
- **Opener:** *"Asked for by name, and by the night he was born. That’s a strange thing to ask for."*

**Tonight's question:** *How did a stranger know the night the boy was born?*
- **A:** From an old record kept somewhere far from here.
- **B:** Someone from this valley told him.
- **Read-back at Gathering 2:** At the Ledger scene, *"Page after page of children's names... and beside each, a date"* —
  > *At the mill [Friend Name] called it: "From an old record kept somewhere far from here."*

**Tonight's puzzle:** *Did he hesitate once he reached the house?*
- **Click pair:** 2-2-1 (The paper in his fist) + 2-2-2 (Straight to the bed)
- **Click text:**  
  > *"The mud on the floorboards runs in one unbroken line from the door to the child's bed, and the paper was in his fist before he crossed the threshold. He did not search the house."*

**Garrick's Nudge:**  
*"{friend:Craft}, look at that footing stone again. The wheel is fifty years old, but the mark under it is older than the valley. How long has that mark been waiting?"*

**Fragment 2-2 Meaning Question:**

## Day 4 · The shaft in the hillside
