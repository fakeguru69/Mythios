# Fan Out and the Campfire: a proposal (14 Oct 2026)

**Status: APPROVED by Fake Guru, 14 Oct 2026** ("approve as per your suggestion, think it through deeply"). The detailed design is in Part 3. New text (clues, questions) still comes to him line by line before it ships. It builds on canon `docs/canon/mythios-campfire-table-v2.md` (v2.3) and keeps its rules:
- stored text only, with no AI replies at the fire
- Garrick never grades a theory
- no gold for theories
- 2 to 4 minutes
- the day is complete without the Campfire
- 2 to 6 friends, nothing assumes four

It changes one 9 Oct decision ("one clue per day, both branches land on the same clue"), following Fake Guru's 14 Oct choice (D6): **each friend who fans out finds a different piece.**

## Fake Guru's brief (14 Oct)

- **Fan Out** has two jobs: the async **regroup** (friends who played at different times come back together at the camp), and **finding new clues** to bring to the Campfire discussion.
- **The Campfire** adds organic play with Garrick and with the other friends, live or async: solving the Seeker Fragment together, discussing what it means, discussing what happened today and this week, and building a theory of the mystery so far.

## The problem today (from the audits)

- Everyone who fans out finds the **same** clue, and the board shows it to everyone anyway. Nobody brings anything only they saw, so there's little to talk about.
- Fan Out is a second encounter (a roll or a generic "rear-guard" fight) with the clue's name on the button.
- The Campfire is 8 steps in a fixed order, mostly "Next".
- The fragment can be solved alone in the Bag at any time, so it's not a shared moment.
- The question of the night (the Fork) is asked twice, once privately before the fire.
- The day's Pull is never shown, so the evening doesn't end on tomorrow.

## Part 1: Fan Out, "the last light"

### What the player does
After the day's main beat, Garrick (or the narrator on W1D1) says the camp is set and there's light for one more look. The player picks **one lead** from the day's leads:
- 3 leads per day
- shown as places or things at the landmark ("the bracken where he waited", "the rock's dry shelf", "the cart-ruts")
- **never the clue's name**

Or they go straight to the fire.

- **One quick action, no fight.** A single Challenge in that lead's own canon verb ("Aim · follow the ruts until they turn"). One tap, using the same canon roll as everything else.
- **The clue always comes back.** The roll only decides whether you also get the first-letter hint for the day's Fragment. Evidence is never gated on a roll (canon clue rule 7: "the story does not depend on it"). On a fail, the stored line says you found it late, in the dark, for example.
- **Each lead is one stored clue.** Friends who pick the same lead find the same clue; the board shows it as one card with both names (canon §M).

### Why three leads works for 2 to 6 friends
- **2 friends:** at most two leads are found; one stays unvisited. The board shows "Nobody went to {the lead's place} tonight." That is a reason to talk ("what would we have found?") and to fan out tomorrow.
- **3 to 6 friends:** every lead can be covered; duplicates merge into one card.
- **Alone at the fire (async):** your lead is on the board; the others appear as friends arrive.

### Content needed
The three leads per day come mostly from what canon already has:
1. **The existing Fan Out clue** (36 written, canon v2.3).
2. **The detail from the "look" choice the player didn't take**, on days that have one (W1D1, W2D2, W3D5, W4D3, W6D3), and canon's [PRESS ON] details once [PRESS ON] is built into the day. Where a [PRESS ON] detail is already the Fan Out clue (12 days), the day gets a new clue in its place.
3. **New clues where canon has none:** about 36–50 short clues, each following canon's seven clue tests (new; a thing you can see, hear or count; narrows the Fork without answering it; respects first-use days; never points at Garrick; one or two sentences; the story doesn't depend on it). Each needs Fake Guru's approval.

**Ideally, each day's three leads lean different ways:** one toward Reading A, one toward B, one that complicates both. Then friends who went different ways arrive with evidence for different theories, and the Fork becomes a real argument.

## Part 2: The Campfire, "a fire you sit at, not a form"

Replace the 8 fixed steps with **one screen around the fire**, with a few things to do in any order. Garrick's stored lines frame it. A friend can do one thing and leave, or stay for all of it.

### 1. Arriving (always first, one screen)
- Garrick's **opener** (canon v2.3, unchanged).
- **Who's been here:** at most 2 names in the catch-up line (canon §M), plus any **message in a bottle** a friend left.
- **The board:** today's clue cards under each friend's name, plus "Nobody went to…" for unvisited leads.

### 2. The Fragment, together (the shared puzzle)
- The word-lock moves to the fire (and stays in the Bag).
- **Canon's rule restored:** each friend may fill **one** of the two words per fragment until dawn; after that, anyone may fill what's left. So a fragment is normally finished by two friends together on its own day.
- **The Fan Out hint helps:** a friend who won their lead roll sees the first letter of word 1.
- **The live moment:** when the second word lands, Garrick (stored line) reads the whole fragment aloud once. The party chat posts "Mei solved a word — one left" (canon party-chat line), which pulls the others back.
- **Then the meaning question:** one stored question per fragment, for example 1-3: "*Some locks already know their key.* Whose key?" A friend may pin a one-line answer. No grading. **These meaning pins are read back at the Gathering** when that fragment is quoted back ("On Day 2, Mei thought…"). This ties the evening to the weekly gut-punch (Point 7).
- New content: 36 meaning questions, one line each.

### 3. Tonight's question (the Fork)
- Canon v2.3's A / B / Neither, plus your own line, asked **once**, here. The private pre-fire ballot is removed (D7).
- Garrick pins it with your name and asks canon's "What would change your mind?"
- **The board shows names, never a winner** (canon v2.3).
- **The 10 weak forks** found in Point 6 are rewritten first, ideally so each leans on that day's leads.

### 4. The case so far (weekly)
- A **week board** with one big question per week (for example Week 1: "Who chose us, and why?"). Each friend keeps one editable line, their current theory. Updating it takes one tap from tonight's pin.
- **Read back at the Gathering's opening** (canon: theory pins are read back there). At the reveal, everyone sees what they believed on Day 1 and on Day 6. No points, no grading. The reveal grades itself.
- New content: 6 week questions.

### 5. Light things (optional)
- **The riddle:** shared, 3 tries each, quiet days on request (canon v2.3).
- **Cup nights:** an action only (canon).
- **Garrick's running gag of the week** (canon).

### 6. Leaving the fire (always last)
- **"What we did today":** one line per friend, only for what they really did (canon).
- **The day's Pull**, read at the fire. This is canon's line that is currently never shown, and it becomes the last thing every friend reads.
- **Message in a bottle** for whoever comes next.

### Live and async
- **Async (the normal case):** everything stays until the dawn cutoff (5:00am party time, canon). Each arrival sees what the others left. The party chat's one-liners ("Dev pinned a theory", "Mei solved a word — one left") bring people back.
- **Live (two or more friends at the fire at once):** a small "at the fire now: Mei, Dev" line. Pins and solved words appear as they happen. Two friends racing for the same word is canon ("two players racing one word").
- **Garrick** says only stored lines (opener, acknowledgements, the fragment reading, "What would change your mind?", the Pull, cup nights, gags). He never answers a theory and never writes a line for a friend.

### In the demo (one phone, D1)
The pretend friends' leads, words and pins appear as placeholders, clearly marked as standing in for real players (canon v2.3: their canned lines are placeholders, not canon speech). A workbench switch sets the party to 2–6 so each size can be tested.

## Part 3: Detailed design (14 Oct, after approval)

### 3.1 The party day, async first

- **A party day runs until 5:00am party time** (canon dawn cutoff). Each friend plays their chapter whenever they like, then fans out, then may sit at the fire. Nothing waits for anyone else.
- **Leads are never used up.** Any friend can follow any lead. "Nobody went to…" only means nobody chose it.
- **Pins are open until dawn,** then lock. A friend who arrives after dawn can still read the board.
- **A missed day** stays readable. The next morning's recap adds one line about what the party found ("Last night the fire heard about the second ring and the leaned bar."), using the stored clue titles only.
- **Day 7 has no Campfire**; the Gathering takes its place.
- **W1D1** is played alone and the party has not met: canon's narrated fire, no Garrick, no friends' leads, no nudge to a friend, and the Fork kept as a private note that becomes your first week-board line.

### 3.2 Fan Out leads

Each day has **three leads**. Each lead is stored data:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `place` | What the button shows: a place or thing at the landmark ("the bracken where he waited"). Never the clue's name. |
| `attrs` | The two Attributes that suit it, each with **its own verb** ("Aim · follow the ruts until they turn"). |
| `clue` | The stored clue: one or two sentences, passing canon's seven clue tests. |
| `leans` | A, B or both, against that evening's Fork. QA only, never shown. |
| `hintFor` | Which of the day's two fragment words this lead's letter hint helps (1 or 2). |
| `lateLine` | One line for a failed roll ("You find it by lamplight, after the others have gone."). |

- **Mix of leans:** each day has one lead leaning A, one leaning B, and one that complicates both. Friends who went different ways arrive with evidence for different readings.
- **Hints split across the two words:** two leads help word 1, one helps word 2. Two friends who go different ways can finish the fragment together more easily, and the mechanics reward splitting up.
- **The roll:** one tap, the same Challenge rule as the day, against the week's DC.
  - On a pass you get the clue and the letter hint.
  - On a fail you get the clue with its `lateLine` and no hint.
  - No gold for now (the clue is the reward; the numbers pass can add some), no Attribute growth (canon), and nothing to fight.
- **Head straight to the fire** stays available. It finishes the day (canon).
- **Where the leads come from:**
  1. the existing 36 Fan Out clues
  2. the unchosen "look" detail on days that have one
  3. new clues, written per day, each line approved by Fake Guru

  Days whose clue is a [PRESS ON] detail keep it as a lead only until [PRESS ON] is built into the day; then that lead gets a new clue so nothing is shown twice.

### 3.3 The fire, as one screen

**Garrick's line budget** (canon: at most 8 to one friend per evening):

| Moment | Lines |
|---|---|
| Opener | 1 |
| Catch-up ("Mei thinks A.") | 1 |
| Reading the finished fragment | 1 |
| Acknowledging your pin ("What would change your mind?") | 1 |
| Week-board prompt | 1 |
| Riddle and its reply | 1–2 |
| The Pull, as he banks the fire | 1 |
| **Total** | **7–8** |

**The parts, in any order after arriving:**

1. **Arriving** (always shown):
   - the opener
   - at most 2 names in the catch-up line
   - any message in a bottle
   - the board: today's clue cards under the finders' names (one card per lead, several names allowed), plus "Nobody went to {place}" for unvisited leads
2. **The Fragment.** The same puzzle and state as the Bag (solving in either place counts). Each friend may fill one of the two words until dawn; then anyone may fill the rest (canon §6). When the second word lands:
   - Garrick reads the whole line once
   - the **meaning question** appears ("*Some locks already know their key.* Whose key?")
   - a friend may pin a one-line answer, editable until the Gathering
3. **Tonight's question:** A, B, Neither, or your own line, once per friend per day, editable until dawn. The board shows names under each reading and never a count or a winner (canon v2.3).
4. **The case so far:** the week's one big question, with each friend's current one-line theory. Editing takes one tap from tonight's pin. The game keeps a dated copy each day, so the Gathering can show how a theory changed.
5. **Light things:** the riddle (shared, 3 tries each, opt-in on quiet days), the cup on cup nights (an action, no line), the week's running gag.
6. **Leaving:** "What we did today" (only real actions), **the Pull**, and a message in a bottle.

### 3.4 Read-backs at the Gathering (the payoff)

- **Who reads them:** MYTHIOS, the narrator. Never Garrick. He doesn't comment on theories, so he never has to lie about one.
- **At the opening:** the week board, each friend's latest line, and where it changed, their Day 1 line ("On Day 1, Priya thought the list was a warning. Tonight: an invitation."). Days are named by number, never by weekday, since a party may start on any day (Fake Guru, 15 Oct).
- **At each fragment quote-back:** up to 3 meaning pins beside the line (canon §M: at most 3 read aloud). They rotate across the season so every friend is read.
- **No grading, ever.** The reveal speaks for itself.

### 3.5 Live play

- "At the fire now: Mei, Dev" while two or more friends are on the screen together.
- Pins, solved words and the riddle update as they happen. Two friends racing one word is canon; the first correct entry wins and the other sees it land.
- The party chat gets short one-liners ("Dev pinned a theory.", "Mei solved a word: one left."). These bring async friends back.
- Friends' own words stay in the party chat. The fire holds only pins and stored lines.

### 3.6 The demo on one phone (D1)

- The pretend friends (Mei, Dev, Arjun, Priya, Theo, depending on the party-size switch) have leads, words and pins shown as "earlier tonight" and marked as samples. They're placeholders for real players, never canon speech.
- The party-size switch (2–6) lets each size be checked: no repeated nudge name, at most 3 pins read aloud, no "four", one card per lead.

### 3.7 Content to write (each line to Fake Guru for approval)

| Content | Amount | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| New Fan Out leads | about 36–50 clues, plus a `place` label and two verbs per lead | canon's seven clue tests; the first-use map; never point at Garrick |
| Fragment meaning questions | 36 | one line; never give the answer; Week 6 questions must not lead toward "royal blood" (canon) |
| Week questions | 6 | one line each, from the season reveal map |
| Fork rewrites | the 10 weak forks (Point 6) | each should lean on that day's leads |
| `lateLine`s | a small rotating set | one line each |

A checker script runs on all of it: first-use words, no party counts or fixed names, lean mix per day, length, no Garrick.

### 3.8 Build order

1. The single-screen fire using today's content (opener, board, Fork, callback, riddle, what we did), plus **the Pull**. Remove the private ballot (D7).
2. The fragment at the fire with one word per friend, the reading aloud, and meaning pins (questions shown as "to be written" until approved).
3. The week board and the Gathering read-backs (needs Phase 3).
4. Fan Out leads: the mechanics first, using the existing clue as lead 1 and the "look" details where they exist; new leads added as approved, Week 1 first.
5. Live presence and party-chat one-liners.

**Depends on:** Phase 1 (the roll), Phase 2 (the Pull and the "look" choices) and Phase 4 (the party list). Steps 1 and 2 can start right after Phase 1.
