# Episode 3 — The Fire Stone

**Investigation:** A third name — a girl called Wren, carried off to "somewhere warm". Somewhere warm turns out to be home: the burned country where she was born.
**The debunk (revealed at the Gathering):** Not a faith mourning a dead king. He isn't dead — he sleeps, put there by the Seekers, whose blood is yours — and the Order, whose faithful cannot lie, is bleeding children to wake him.

**New places this week:** **Ashcombe**, a shepherds' village where the green hills give out and the black ones begin. **The Blackglass**, a shoulder of hillside above it where the rock once ran like wax and cooled into black glass — the Fire trail's find-site. **The Seekers' shelter**, a room cut into a black ridge. **The Kiln**, a round black hill with a crack across its crown that breathes warm air — the Gathering's site.

## Day 2. Ashcombe

*Heading: **Ashcombe.***

**What you notice**

## Day 4. The black ridges

*Heading: **The black ridges.***
*Question: is she still alive?*

> The burned country is ridge after ridge of black rock, under a sky that looks paler for it. The soil between the ridges is ash-grey, and crunches like old snow. Dead trees stand bone-white, still upright after nobody knows how long.
>
> No bird sings out here. By noon your own footsteps are the loudest thing for miles.
>
> You are walking point when you stop, and crouch.
>
> In one of the hoof-scratches lies a chip of black glass, worn smooth by a thumb.

**What you notice**
