## Part 1. The 12 stats

Each stat is a number, bigger is better, something the player chose to do, and a thing the game says on screen when it happens. On the Record page they sit on **one ruled scoreboard plate, four across in three rows** (thin dividing lines, no separate tiles), so the section looks different from Gear and Badges (changed 5 Oct 2026, TV).

### Row 1 · SHOW UP
| Stat | What the number is | What the game says when it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Days Played** | Total days you read the chapter to its last line, over the season. | "Mei has played today." |
| **Best Streak** | Your longest run of days played in a row. It never goes down. | On your summary card: "Streak: 4 days." |
| **Gatherings Attended** | Gatherings you came to. | The lobby shows who is in. |
| **Fragments Solved** | Fragments where you filled a missing word. | "Mei solved a word of today's fragment." |

### Row 2 · TOGETHER
| Stat | What the number is | What the game says when it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Relics Given** | Relics you gave to a friend, of any kind. | "Dev gave Mei a Charm Relic." |
| **Times Stepped Up** | Times you tapped [STEP UP] and rolled for the party. | "Mei stepped up." |
| **Fragments Matched** | Fragments you matched to the right scripture line at a Gathering. | "MATCH." then "Mei and Jo matched it." |
| **Battles Won** | Gathering Battles the party won while you fought in them. | "VICTORY." |

### Row 3 · GLORY
| Stat | What the number is | What the game says when it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenges Cleared** | Every Challenge you cleared. | "Mei cleared today's Challenge." |
| **Challenges Pressed On** | Times you took the harder [PRESS ON] roll, cleared or not. | "Mei pressed on." |
| **Enemies Defeated** | Enemies your own hand brought down, in weekday fights and Battles. | "VICTORY." |
| **Perfect Rolls** | Each 20 you rolled on a Challenge or an attack. | "PERFECT ROLL." |

A small crown sits on any stat where you lead the party. Nothing ever goes down, and no stat records a miss.

**Dropped:** Finds (optional play is cut), Gold Earned (Days Played in disguise), Fastest Fight and Closest Call (a smaller number was better, with no unit), Joined In (automatic), Rescues (overlapped Relics Given), Joint Efforts won (the whole party shares it; it is on the party board).

## Part 2. The 12 badges

A badge is a medal that turns up when something worth telling happens. Nobody has to read a list to chase one: the party is told in one line when it is earned. Four across, three rows. An unearned badge shows **greyed out with its name**, so the grid stays even and the player can see what exists. Tapping any badge, earned or not, gives the explainer: what it is for and what earns it. Nobody has to read the grid to chase one; the grey slots are a quiet nudge, and the party is told in one line when one is earned. A badge pays no gold.

### Row 1 · SHOW UP
| Badge | Given when | The game says |
|---|---|---|
| **First Light** | You finish your first chapter. | "BADGE: First Light. Mei has begun." |
| **A Whole Week** | You play all six days and come to the Gathering in one week. | "BADGE: A Whole Week. Mei missed nothing." |
| **The Long Road** | You come to all six Gatherings. | "BADGE: The Long Road." |
| **Sharpened** | Any of your Attributes reaches **Adept** (a tier shown on your Record page). | "BADGE: Sharpened. Mei's Craft is Adept." |

### Row 2 · TOGETHER
| Badge | Given when | The game says |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Hand** | You give a friend a Relic for the first time. | "BADGE: Open Hand." |
| **Lifeline** | A Relic you gave is used in a roll or a fight that then goes the friend's way. | "BADGE: Lifeline. Dev's Relic carried Mei." |
| **The Whole Table** | Every friend plays on the same day. The whole party earns it. | "BADGE: The Whole Table. All of you, today." |
| **A Clean Night** | In one Gathering the party clears the opening roll, wins the Battle with nobody down, and wins the United Effort. The whole party earns it. | "BADGE: A Clean Night." |

### Row 3 · GLORY
| Badge | Given when | The game says |
|---|---|---|
| **Perfect** | You roll your first 20. | "BADGE: Perfect." |
| **Never Enough** | You clear three [PRESS ON] rolls in one week. | "BADGE: Never Enough." |
| **Many Hands** | You clear Challenges with four different Attributes. | "BADGE: Many Hands." |
| **Down to a Thread** | You win a fight with 10 HP or less left. | "BADGE: Down to a Thread. Mei won with 4 HP." |

Two of the twelve belong to the whole party. Those are the ones friends will talk about.

## Part 4. The async problem: how a day ends and the next begins

Three cards, each with one job.

1. **End of every chapter: your own summary card** (private). Your gold, growth, streak, the one nudge line. It cannot show what friends do later.
2. **Next morning, on sign-in: one group card, "The Party, Yesterday".** It covers what the friends did since you last looked.
3. **Then today's chapter.**

**Who sees what, in order:**
- **You played yesterday:** The Party, Yesterday, then today's chapter.
- **You missed one or more days:** the recap of the missed day(s) first (Core Rules: at most five lines per missed day, ending on the Pull), then **one** Party, Yesterday card covering everything since you last signed in, then today's chapter. One card, never one per missed day.
- **Gathering mornings are not special.** The Week's Best is read in the Gathering lobby that evening, so Day 1's morning card still appears and covers the Gathering night.

> **THE PARTY, YESTERDAY**
> Mei rolled a PERFECT ROLL.
> Dev gave Jo a Charm Relic.
> TV pressed on.
> BADGE: Open Hand. Dev.
> Three of four played. One more makes a Whole Table.
> *Your streak: 4 days. Your best is 5.*

Rules:
- **Only good news.** It lists what friends did, never what anyone missed.
- **Up to five lines, best first:** a badge, a Perfect Roll, a Relic given, a crown changing hands, then who played.
- **It never repeats** a line you already saw in the party chat or on the ticker.
- Everything on it comes from the party chat's own one-line lines, so nothing new is tracked.
- If nothing happened, there is no card.

## Part 6. What "the Week's Best" is (your question 6)

It is the bragging moment at the start of each Gathering. While the friends arrive in the lobby, MYTHIOS reads **one line for every friend present: the best true thing that friend did that week**, picked from their own stats.

> *This week. Mei pressed on four times. Dev gave two Relics. Jo solved five fragments. TV cleared every Challenge.*

Why it works: every friend is named, so everyone gets a moment; nobody is last, because each gets their own best stat (if two friends would get the same one, the second gets their next best); and it takes twenty seconds, with everyone in the room at once, so nobody has to open a page to see it.

At the **end of the season** the same idea gives each friend one **Season Title** for their best stat: Steady Feet (Days Played), The Unbroken (Best Streak), The Faithful (Gatherings Attended), The Reader (Fragments Solved), The Open Hand (Relics Given), First Forward (Times Stepped Up), The Keen Eye (Fragments Matched), The Wall (Battles Won), The Sure Hand (Challenges Cleared), The Bold (Challenges Pressed On), The Hunter (Enemies Defeated), The Lucky One (Perfect Rolls).

**The party's board** (one view, always open): Gatherings with everyone present · Whole Table days · United Efforts won · Matches with everyone right.

## Part 7. How Match works on the chat screen (TV's question 7)

**Today's rule sends the player to the Bag** ("each friend's Seeker Chest opens on the week's six") and shows the picks after 20 seconds. That is a real weakness: a trip into another tab in the middle of a live Gathering, and 20 seconds is too short to read six fragments. **Decided: no trip to the Bag, ever.** The whole thing happens in the party chat.

**What the player sees**
1. MYTHIOS posts a **MATCH** card in the party chat, showing the scripture line in full.
2. Under it, six rows, one per fragment found that week. Each row has the day and place it was found ("Day 3, the ferry shed") and its first few words. Tap a row and the full fragment opens right there, in the card.
3. Each row has a **[PICK]** button. The pick is private: nobody sees it until the reveal.
4. A small line shows who has picked ("Mei, Dev have picked"), never what.
5. When all are in, or the clock runs out, the right row lights up with the initials of those who matched it (a wrong pick is never shown) and the result reads "Mei and Jo matched it."

**The clock:** 20 seconds is too short to read six rows. Decided: **60 seconds**, ending early the moment every friend present has picked. A friend who has not picked is simply not counted; nobody is blamed.

**Why this is safe:** the fragments are short quotes (the registry lists 36), and every one is already in the Bag from the weekdays, so the card only repeats what the player has already read. The Bag copy stays for reading at leisure; Match never needs it.
