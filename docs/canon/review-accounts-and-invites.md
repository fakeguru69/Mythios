# Accounts and Invites — from the link to Day 1

**Status: APPROVED by TVMen and WRITTEN into Core Rules §12a, 2026-10-03.** Changes at approval: there is no age check at all; the host names the party; the invite is headed "Join the adventure in Tales of the Dark Moon"; a person may be in several parties; a name can be changed at any time.

This covers everything a person sees before the story starts, and what happens when someone leaves. It follows the decisions already made (Core Rules §12a): sign-in by Google, Apple, or email and password; the host approves each friend; the host can remove a friend; the game is free.

Two rules shaped every screen:

- **Nothing here gives the story away.** A new player learns only what the first minute of Day 1 will show them.
- **Nothing reassures before a risk.** No screen says "you can't lose".

## 3. The rules behind these screens

**The invite link**
- One link per party. It works until the party has six, or until the end of Episode 1 Day 2, whichever comes first.
- Tapping it only asks to join. The host lets each person in.
- The host can make a new link at any time; the old one stops working.

**After the link closes**
> **This party has set off.**
>
> Their story has started, and nobody can join it now. You can start a party of your own.
>
> [START A PARTY]

**A party that is full**
> **This party is full.**
> Six is as many as the road will hold.

**Turned away by the host**
> **Not this time.**
> TV hasn't let you into this party.

No reason is given, and the friend is not told twice.

**A friend who joins after Day 1 has opened** plays Day 1 first, then the current day (Core Rules §12a).

## 4. Leaving, being removed, and a new host

**Leaving, by choice** (in the party's settings)
> **Leave the party?**
>
> Your friends will carry on without you. You can't come back to this story.
>
> [LEAVE]  [STAY]

**What the party sees** (one line in the party chat)
> *Dev has left the road.*

**Removing a friend** (host only)
> **Remove Dev from the party?**
>
> They will lose their place in this story and can't come back to it.
>
> [REMOVE]  [KEEP]

The party chat shows the same line as for leaving: *"Dev has left the road."* The removed friend sees:

> **You're no longer in this party.**

**In the story,** a friend who has gone is simply no longer counted. No scene explains it, and no character remarks on it.

**If the host leaves,** the friend who joined first becomes host, and the party chat says:
> *Mei now leads the party.*

**The last friend leaves:** the party ends.

## 5. Reporting

Friends play with people they know, so this is kept small.

- **Press and hold any message** in the party chat → **[REPORT]** → *"Tell us what's wrong."* (a short box) → **[SEND]**.
- The report comes to us with that message and the few around it. The person reported is not told.
- The host's power to remove a friend is the first remedy. A report is for when the host is the problem, or will not act.
- Private threads cannot be reported by friends, because friends cannot see them.

---

## 6. Deleting an account

In settings:

> **Delete your account?**
>
> Everything of yours is erased. In any story you were part of, your friends will still see your name where it was, and nothing else.
>
> [DELETE]  [KEEP]

## 7. Decided at approval

1. A person may be in several parties at once, with a separate character in each.
2. The host names the party.
3. A name can be changed at any time; the party chat shows the change once.
4. The invite is headed "Join the adventure in Tales of the Dark Moon".
5. There is no age check.
