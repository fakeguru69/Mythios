# Mythios — Glossary: one word for each thing

**Status: DECIDED AND APPLIED, 5 Oct 2026 (the one word list for everything a player sees; any new player-facing word must come from here). Revised twice the same day after TV's reviews. Where this file and `mythios-stats-and-badges-proposal.md` (fifth pass, the current one) differ on a stat or badge name, that file is the current one. Optional play was cut on 5 Oct, so Find, note, Lure, Bonus and free play are no longer words in the game at all.** It has been applied (see the master tracker). It answers: "which words does a player see, which are only ours, and can we make them obvious and fewer?"

## The honest answer to "is United Effort player-facing?"

**No. Today it is a word only we use.** The player never sees it. They see a scene (THE DROP, THE HAWSER) and two bars. The word "job" I used last time is also a script stage direction, not text the player reads. So my "Jobs Done" was wrong: it invented a second name for something that has no player-facing name yet. Good catch.

That is the real problem, and it is bigger than one word. The rules, the scripts and the Card use different words for the same thing in several places, and some words are ours alone. Below is every term, sorted into what the player sees and what stays behind the screen.

## The rule I used

1. **One thing, one word.** If a player can see it, it has exactly one name, everywhere.
2. **Plain English first.** A word is good if a stranger could guess it. If not, it is explained once, at the moment it first happens, in one line, by MYTHIOS.
3. **Our words stay ours.** Words like "beat", "Lure", "Approach", "DC" and "safety net" are for us and the builder. They never appear on a screen.
4. **No new word where an everyday one works.**

## Part 3. The three decisions

### 1. The shared job: keep "United Effort" (TV, 5 Oct)
Decided by TV: **United Effort keeps its name.** My "Joint Effort" suggestion is withdrawn and nothing is renamed. It goes on the card as **UNITED EFFORT · THE DROP**, and the first time one appears (Gathering 1) MYTHIOS says one line: *"This is a United Effort. One job, and you take it in turns."* (corrected 5 Oct, see Part 5) It has no personal count on the Card; "United Efforts won" is on the party's board.

### 2. "Connect" is not obvious: rename to "Match"
The word means nothing on its own, and "connect" also means connecting to the internet or to friends. What the player does is match a line of scripture to the right fragment. **Match** says exactly that. TV approved. It runs in the party chat as a MATCH card with six fragment rows and [PICK] buttons, 60 seconds, no trip to the Bag. The summary card reads **Matched 2 of 2**. In the rules, "Connect" becomes "Match" (35 places). The stat is **Fragments Matched** (TV's choice).

### 3. "Episode" vs "Week": DECIDED, Week (TV, 5 Oct 12:21)
The rules say "A season is episodes, an episode is 7 days" but the scripts and the player say "Week 6 Day 1". Two words for the same span invites confusion. Proposal: **the player-facing word is Week.** "Episode" stays as the story's title word (the six files are "episodes"; each Week has a name like "The Fire Stone"). In the Honours I wrote "in one episode"; those say "in one Week".
