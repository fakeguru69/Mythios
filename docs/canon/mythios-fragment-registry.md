# Tales of the Dark Moon — Lore Fragment Registry

**What this document is:** the tracking table for all Seeker Fragment lore text across the season — 6 per episode, **36 total (6 episodes, season locked at 6)**. Every fragment appears here with what it seeds, its Closure episode, and any risk flagged, so a Closure can verify it's paying off real setup rather than reconstructing from memory.

**All 36 are locked (2026-09-28).** Episode 1 was reviewed and applied 2026-09-26. Episodes 2-6 went through the full relook (`review-fragments.md`) and were approved by TVMen 2026-09-28. The per-Gathering context — which reveal each fragment serves and how it's re-quoted — lives in `mythios-season-reveal-map.md`.

**Authorship, confirmed:** Seeker Fragments are literally written by the original Seekers (their number is never stated to players — TVMen, 2026-09-28), in the immediate aftermath of the battle with Malakor — a primary historical record, not cult or Meira authorship. **They never quote the ancient text**, even though the Seekers knew it in full (locked 2026-09-23). They also can't know the future — so they can set up the *lore* half of a Gathering (the Lore Reveal), never the present-day half (the Twist), which is always set up by in-scene evidence.

**Mandatory rule:** at Closure, the payoff scene must explicitly re-quote the fragment's exact original words — never just gesture at "you understand now." *(Relaxed 2026-09-29, series audit: each Gathering now re-quotes only its three strongest fragments. The others still close at their listed episode — the reveal they set up lands there — and are shown beside their payoff in the Recap.)*

**TVMen's fragment rule (2026-09-27) — GM Principles 2.10:** every fragment is either **setup** (an important piece of the lore, planted so the Gathering's reveal clicks when it's quoted back) or **drama** (a vivid, emotional moment from the lore, in the Seekers' voice). A seed that pays off later is allowed only if it carries a finale thread and reads plainly at its payoff. Colour that is neither doesn't earn a fragment. **The full relook of all 36 against this rule (`review-fragments.md`) was approved by TVMen 2026-09-28 and is applied below.**

**Seeding principle, confirmed 2026-09-23 (restated by TVMen the same day: "seeker fragments to (mostly) setup the episode reveal (or later ones)"):**
- **Most fragments set up their own episode's reveal** — planted and paid off close together.
- **A minority seed later reveals** — chiefly Layer F (false hope) and the two finale threads (moon-blood; "the throne rise whole"). These are meant to reward a reread, not telegraph anything in the moment.
- **Episode 1 is the exception to "most":** it has no Lore Reveal, so all six of its fragments are seeds for later.
- **Episode 6 has an extra constraint:** none of its fragments may let the party work out "only royal blood works" before the Gathering, or they'd never hand Garrick the fifth.

**The rule (Core Rules §6):** each fragment is its own puzzle. It shows its own text with two words missing, their letters jumbled. Each word is its own lock worth 25g; a player may fill one word per fragment until the day ends, then anyone may fill what is left.

**How the words were chosen (TVMen: "find words that are critical for understanding"):** the two words that carry the fragment's meaning, so that solving it is what makes it make sense. Each chosen word appears only once in its fragment, so the visible text never gives it away. Where a fragment turns on a pair (gives / takes, locks / unlocks, woke / wake, four / five), the pair is the lock.
