**Investigation:** A second name, a second child, and a working mill two days southeast.
**The debunk (revealed at the Gathering):** Not a kidnapper, not a ring. A faith — old, patient, and still keeping count — that gives the blood of marked children to a king the world tried to forget. And the party itself is marked.

## Day 2. The mill

*Heading: **The mill.***
*Question: what happened at the mill?*

> The mill sits an hour on, in a fold of the valley where the stream has been made to work. You hear it before you see it: the slow knock and splash of the wheel.

*The lane:*
> Ruts in, and ruts out, four nights old. One cart. It did not turn round in the yard. Somebody led the horse about by its head, so as not to make a sound.

## Day 3. Upriver

*Heading: **Upriver.***
*Question: where did the cart go?*

> The rain came in the night. By morning the ruts are running with water, and you follow them upriver at a pace that leaves no breath for talk.
>
> The river has risen in the dark. It runs fat and loud beside you, and the rain still comes in fits off the hills, cold on the back of the neck.
>
> The tracks hold until the riverbank turns to stone. Then they stop.
>
> There is no ford here. There is no reason for a cart to have come this far.

**What you notice**
> **Might** · The thorn on the left has been cut back and tied, not broken. *Somebody tends it.*
> **Agility** · No bird is singing on the slope above. *Something up there has kept them off for days.*
> **Intellect** · A cart does not vanish. It was unloaded here, or it went up. *There is nowhere to unload.*
> **Charm** · In the one patch of mud, a child's shoe-print, pointing uphill. *He walked this part.*
> **Craft** · The left wheel pulled. *It left the bank at the grey slab.*
> **Aim** · High on the slope there is a line where the bracken lies the wrong way. *A path, seen from the side.*

**Challenge card**
> **WHERE THE TRACKS STOP**

> **Agility** · slip through the thorn, by its gaps, across what looks like an old path · `[ROLL]`
> REWARD: +500g

*On a pass:*

*On a failure:*

## Day 4. What the hillside hides

*Heading: **The end of the path.***
*Question: what is at the end of the hidden path?*

> The path climbs for an hour through bracken and scree. Up here the wind has an edge to it, and it brings you lamp-smoke before it brings you anything else.
>
> The path ends at a hole in the hill.
>
> It is a shaft, squared off with timber, with a ladder going down into lamplight. There are voices at the bottom.

**What you notice**
> **Might** · The ladder is new ash, pegged, and strong enough for men carrying a load. *They mean to use this place for a long time.*
> **Agility** · The third rung from the top is worn pale on the left. *Everyone who climbs this steps in the same place.*
> **Intellect** · Lamp-smoke, coming up steadily, and no cooking in it. *Nobody lives down there. They work there.*
> **Charm** · The voices are not arguing, and they are not laughing. *They are saying the same thing, together.*
> **Craft** · The shaft is old. The timbering is this year's. *Somebody found it, and opened it again.*
> **Aim** · Straight down: a lamp, a patch of floor, and the edge of something that is not floor. *A foot. Small, and bare.*

**Challenge card**
> **DOWN THE SHAFT**
> One ladder. Whatever is down there, you will see it for as long as you are not seen.
> **Agility** · go down without a sound · `[ROLL]`
> **Charm** · go down as if you belong, past the one posted at its foot · `[ROLL]`
> REWARD: +500g
> IF YOU FAIL: you are seen from the top, and you see almost nothing

*On a pass:*
> You are halfway down before anyone looks up, and for the length of three breaths you see all of it. The air coming up past you is warm, and smells of tallow and wet stone.
>
> A chamber cut into the hill. A ring of lamps set on the floor. Hooded figures, more of them than there are of you, moving round the ring without hurry, setting things in their places.
>
> And in the middle, on his back on the stone, a boy. His wrists are tied. His hands lie open, palms up, and there is not a mark on them.

*On a failure:*
> A lamp swings up the shaft while you are still at the top, and a watchman's eyes meet yours.

> Behind him: lamplight, and hooded shapes, and nothing you could swear to. But under the voices there is another sound. A boy, crying, very quietly.

> Garrick is the last to reach you. He sits down in the roots, breathing hard. *"That went about as well as it looked."*

## Day 5. The hillside by night

*Heading: **The hillside, after dark.***
*Question: who are these people, and what are they making ready?*

> Dark comes up out of the valley and over the hill. Far below, at the foot of the shaft, their lamp burns on.
>

`[WALK THE HILL]` `[READ MEIRA'S PAGES]`

*The player does one. Friends do the other, and what they found is shown.*

*Walking the hill:*
> You quarter it until your legs give out. Bracken. Scree. A sheepfold, fallen in. There is no second door.

*Meira's pages:*
> By the last of the light, in a margin, pressed hard into the paper:
>
