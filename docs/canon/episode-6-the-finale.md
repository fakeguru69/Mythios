# Episode 6 — The Finale

**Investigation:** Seven nights until the dark moon. The fifth Stone is in your hands, and the Seekers' own words say where to take it: where he fell, under the dark moon. But his hall is sealed, and the only way in is written in their blood — six more fragments, somewhere in the dead city, before the night comes.
**The debunk (revealed at the Gathering):** Only Garrick's blood wakes the fifth. And the fifth doesn't end the waking. It finishes it.

**New places this week:** **The defenders' barracks**, long halls under the city wall. **The moon-tower**, a slender tower where the old kingdom's sky-watchers kept their records. **The hill behind the hall**, a rise of cracked rock where the city's highest ground runs out.

## Day 1. Six nights

*Heading: **Six nights.***
*Question: he looked straight at the window. Are you found?*

> Nobody breathes.

> In the grey before morning somebody eases the hidden door open a finger's width, to look along the walk.

>

**What you notice**
> **Might** · The hidden door is one slab, a hand thick, hung so that a child could swing it. *It was made to be shut in a hurry, and held.*

> **Aim** · From the slit, the hall's doors sit square in the middle of the view. *This window was cut for one thing.*

*Question: where does the arrow lead?*
