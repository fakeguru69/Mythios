# Episode 4 — The Water Stone

**Investigation:** The fourth child, Joss, is "already with them". The ledger puts his home on the Stillwater, a lake in the north — and under its shallow end lies a village the water drowned long ago.
**The debunk (revealed at the Gathering):** Not blood alone. The children's blood was never meant for the king — it was meant for the Stones, and every Stone was found by you. Then: a fifth Stone exists, and a torn page says the fifth "shall end—" it.

**New places this week:** **Reedham**, a fishing village on the shore of **the Stillwater**, a long cold lake in the north. **Old Reedham**, the village it used to be, drowned when the valley flooded generations ago; in a dry year its streets come up out of the shallow end. **The drowned tower**, Old Reedham's bell tower, still standing out of the water — the Water trail's find-site. **The reed island**, a low hummock of reeds and willow out in the lake.

## Day 4. Old Reedham

*Heading: **Old Reedham.***
*Question: who has been out in the drowned streets?*

> You walk the shore to the shallow end in the morning, and the old village comes up to meet you.
>
> The Stillwater has drawn back far enough to leave Old Reedham's upper streets standing in the open: cobbles slick with weed, doorways full of silt, walls water-marked green to the height of your chest. Everything smells of mud and old rope. A gull stands on a chimney that has no house under it.
>
> Further out, the same streets slope down, and simply go on under the water.

**What you notice**
> **Might** · The water-mark on every wall is level with your chest. *All of this was under the lake, and will be again.*
> **Agility** · The weed is green on every cobble but those down the middle of one lane. *It has been walked on.*
