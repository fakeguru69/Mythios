# Mythios — Core Rules (The Engine)

**Clean edition, 2026-10-03. This file states only the rules in force.** Every earlier version, correction and piece of reasoning is kept in `mythios-core-rules-history.md`. If the two ever disagree, this file is right.

**What this document is:** the engine any Mythios season runs on, whatever its story. Narrative craft is in `mythios-gm-principles.md`; the story of this season (Tales of the Dark Moon) is in the episode files and `mythios-gathering-scripts.md`.

**The dividing line:** if a rule would have to change to run a different story, it is story content and does not belong here. Names (Attributes, classes, gear) may be reskinned per story; numbers and structure may not, without a deliberate, tested reason.

**The one idea behind the numbers:** the world has fixed numbers, the same for every friend, and players grow against it.

---

## 1. The season, the week, and where the game is played

**Season and week**
- A season runs 4 to 8 episodes. Tales of the Dark Moon has 6.
- An episode is 7 real calendar days. **Story day = calendar day.**
- Days 1 to 6 each have one chapter of about five minutes (see below), ending on the Pull and a day summary card. There is no optional play (cut 5 Oct 2026).
- Day 7 is the Gathering: the week's live session, played together.
- A season is started by 2 to 6 friends. Anything that counts the party uses whoever is actually present, so a Gathering can run with fewer, even one.
- **[N]** in a script is the party's size on the day that scene is delivered. Once delivered it is never rewritten.

**The daily beat** has two parts:
- **Plot Resolution:** answers today's question. It holds everything a player needs to follow the plot.
- **The Pull:** names tomorrow's place and why it cannot wait.

**Decided 4 Oct 2026, to be worked through the scripts** (see `review-daily-chapter-audit.md`):
- **One paid beat a day (decided 4 Oct 2026).** Every weekday chapter has one beat that pays gold: a Challenge or a fight. A second roll appears only where the scene has a second real risk. The rest of a day's play is Choices, Talks and acts, and each of those must change something (what the player finds, how the next beat opens, which lead a friend takes). The season plan has 29 Challenges and 12 weekday fights, so five days have both: Week 1 Day 1 (the tutorial teaches each once), and Week 2 Day 3, Week 4 Day 2, Week 4 Day 5 and Week 6 Day 6, where the roll decides how the fight opens. The numbers in §12 and the simulation are to be re-run on that basis. (Count corrected 5 Oct 2026; this line had said 30 and 8.)
- **More game per minute, not more minutes (decided 4 Oct 2026).** Four additions to every weekday chapter, none of which lengthens it:
  - **Your build notices things.** Once or twice a day the chapter has a "notice" line with six versions, one for each Attribute. A player sees the version for their own highest Attribute; from Adept upward it gains a second sentence. A notice line gives extra detail and never a fact the story needs: every player gets the day's answer.
  - **The roll sets up what follows.** A passed Challenge changes the player's position in the next beat: who is standing beside them, whether they are seen, and in a fight, who strikes first or how the enemy starts.
  - **About two weekday fights a week where the story allows** (the season plan has 12, and 29 Challenges). Not every enemy is the Order: beasts, the dead, a chained dog and ordinary thieves give gear and Combat Relics more use without repeating the silent man who runs.
  - **The summary card shows the chase:** the Attribute's old and new number with how far the next tier is, and what the player's gold can now buy.
- **[PRESS ON] (decided 4 Oct 2026).** On about half the Challenge days, a passed Daily Challenge offers one optional harder roll on the same card, with [PRESS ON] and [LEAVE IT]. It is 4 harder than the first roll (the Trial's difficulty in §12). A pass pays extra gold and gives one more detail that the story does not depend on. A failure costs the chance only: no gold is lost and the first reward is kept. An Attribute Relic may be used on it. **Set 5 Oct 2026 (Audit G): a passed press pays +200g and grows nothing.** Growing it as well would give a Daily player 7 more points in their best Attribute for no reason the story gives.
- **The summary card's "You hold" line appears only when the thing held matters to the story** (Wren's chips, the watch-page). It is not an inventory.
- **A weekday chapter takes about five minutes** (four to seven; never more than ten). Set 4 Oct 2026 at seven; lowered 5 Oct 2026 after a paper timing (three usable reports): a Challenge day ran about 4 to 4.5 minutes and a fight day about 6, reading at 200 words a minute, which is quick for a phone. It is one focused chapter with a clear end. A real play was skipped on 5 Oct 2026 (TV); the paper timing stands.
- **The chapter ends with the Pull as its last line, then a day summary card:** what the player earned, what grew, the state of today's fragment, and where tomorrow leads.
- **Keepsakes are dropped, and so is optional play (decided 5 Oct 2026, TVMen: "Let's lose the optional play. It gets complicated.").** There is no Lure, no free play, no Bonus Challenge, no Bonus Fight and no note. A chapter ends on the Pull and the day summary card. The story's own objects (Meira's pen and the like) remain as Scroll Box entries. Talking to Garrick, the party chat and the daily theory prompt are unchanged: they are talk, not play, and never a roll.
- Whether an optional scene survives at all, and what each day's Challenges and fights are, is decided by the daily-chapter rework.

**Front-loading rule:** a player who reads the day's chapter every day can follow the whole season. There is nothing else to play on a weekday.

**The three places**

| Place | What it is | What happens there |
|---|---|---|
| **Your thread** | One player and MYTHIOS, 1:1 | The day's beat, your own Challenge, your fight rounds, private questions to Garrick, the daily theory prompt (one optional question after the beat: what do you think is going on?) |
| **Party chat** | All the friends, MYTHIOS and Garrick | One-line results, Garrick's banter, the friends' own talk, fragment news, help-calls, the map on Day 6, the weekly poll, and the whole Gathering |
| **The tabs** | Record, Bag, Store | Your numbers and badges; what you carry; what you can buy |

**A weekday, in order**
1. The beat arrives in your thread. The day's fragment (§6) is already in every friend's Seeker Chest (§8).
2. You read up to the story's Challenge and roll it yourself. It is your Daily Challenge (§3).
3. Your branch of the scene plays. Both branches reach the same plot.
4. The beat finishes with the Pull.
5. One line goes to the party chat. It is about the friend, not the world ("TV passed today's Challenge."), because two friends may get different branches of the same scene.
6. The day summary card closes the chapter.

The chapter is the whole of a weekday's play.

**The party chat on weekdays** is alive and optional. Garrick is the only character in it, because he is the only one who travels with the party. He replies when spoken to and may add a line or two a day. His scripted lines are the only ones that carry plot; anything else he says follows §11, "The GM's limits". Every other character speaks in the scene where the player meets them, in the player's own thread. The party chat also shows which friends have not played today.

**The Gathering (Day 7, live, in the party chat)** has this shape:

1. **The Approach:** one group Choice, then one Challenge taken by a volunteer and rolled in front of everyone. The Choice decides which Attribute the Challenge uses, so it settles who is best placed to roll.
2. **The Battle:** each friend fights their own enemy. Rounds show in each friend's own thread; status lines and help-calls show in the party chat.
3. **The United Effort:** one per Gathering (§5). It sits here by default and may move in a given episode.
4. **The Lore Reveal,** with questions to Garrick and others (Talk, free). Present only when new lore is due; Episode 1 has none.
5. **The Twist.**
6. **The Cliffhanger.**

Episode 6 keeps its own order of reveals. How a Gathering is scheduled, started and paced is in §11.

**Getting in and out** (decided 4 Oct 2026)
- After signing in, a player lands on the **Library:** the list of game titles. *Tales of the Dark Moon* is one of them.
- Tapping a title opens its **Seasons page:** each season, and under it the player's party or parties in that season with where each one stands. From here a player continues a party, starts one, or joins one by invite.
- Inside a game, the title at the top of the screen is the way out: tapping it returns to the Seasons page. The Seasons page has the same way back to the Library.

## 2. Attributes and growth

**Six Attributes, one per class**

| Attribute | Class | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Might | Warrior | Overpowering force |
| Craft | Artificer | Tools and machines |
| Intellect | Sage | Fast, deep thinking |
| Charm | Trickster | Persuasion, influence |
| Agility | Assassin | Speed, stealth |
| Aim | Archer | Precision |

**Starting values:** every Attribute is 10, except the class Attribute (14) and one chosen second Attribute (12).

**Five tiers, 25 points each**

| Tier | Attribute value | Bonus on a Challenge | Growth rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Novice | 1 to 25 | +0 | Full |
| Apprentice | 26 to 50 | +2 | Full |
| Adept | 51 to 75 | +4 | 60% |
| Expert | 76 to 100 | +6 | 30% |
| Master | 101 and up | +8 | 10% |

The Attribute's number is progress toward the next tier. Only the tier's bonus is added to a roll.

**Growth**
- An Attribute grows when you succeed at a **Daily Challenge** with it (§3).
- The amount depends on how many days you have played that week: **12, 9, 7, 5, 4, 3, 3** for your first to seventh day played. It counts days played, not the calendar, so a friend who plays only Day 1 and Day 5 gets 12 and then 9. This is on purpose: it keeps occasional players close. It resets each week.
- The amount is multiplied by the growth rate of the tier the Attribute is in before the award, then rounded to the nearest whole point, and is never less than 1.
- A Challenge only grows the Attribute if it was truly uncertain: both a roll of 1 failing and a roll of 20 succeeding had to be possible. This is judged before any Relic is added, so using a Relic never costs a player their growth.

## 3. Challenges

**The rule**
- A Challenge is **d20 + the Attribute's tier bonus, against a Difficulty Class (DC).**
- It succeeds when **Roll + bonus − DC is 1 or more.** A tie fails.
- **A DC is one number for the obstacle, the same for every friend and every Attribute.** The DC for each episode is in §12.
- A Challenge that offers two Attributes lets the player pick. The Attribute in the higher tier needs the lower roll; in the same tier they need the same roll.
- The card's "You Succeed On" number is the lowest roll that makes Roll + bonus − DC come to 1 or more.

**The four kinds of moment.** If a card appears, something is at stake. No card, it's free.

| Kind | Example | Roll | Gold | Growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Choice** | Left door or right door; talk to A or B | No | No | No |
| **Talk** | Ask the guard a plain question | No | No | No |
| **Daily Challenge** | Your first Challenge of the day | Yes, with a card | +500g / −150g | Yes |

- **When to roll at all:** only when the attempt could really fail and something is won or lost. A guard with nothing to hide simply answers. Most moments in a long session are Choice or Talk.
- **Daily Challenge:** the story's Challenge in the day's chapter. Every player rolls it in their own thread. A day whose chapter has a fight and no Challenge has no Daily Challenge: the fight is that day's paid beat (§4).
- **No second chance on the same day** (decided 5 Oct 2026). The old safety net gave a second Daily Challenge after a failed one. It needed a second roll to exist, and with optional play gone there is none, so it is retired (§14). It was worth about 500g a season.
- **Day 7:** only the friend who rolls the Approach Challenge has a Daily Challenge. Nobody else has one that day.
- **Story Challenges fail forward.** Both branches reach the same plot. The failure branch must show a visible setback; whether the detour is pulled back is a backend matter the player never sees.
- **A clue missed on a fail goes to the fire** *(15 Oct 2026, Fake Guru)*. Where a pass shows a detail that a fail does not, the day summary card says so on a fail ("You missed something at the ford. Someone at the fire may have seen it."), and at that evening's Campfire a friend who passed shares what they saw. Failing gives a reason to talk, not a silent loss.
- **[NOT NOW]:** the card is set aside. It stays in the thread and can be rolled any time before the day ends, and while it is set aside the Store is open. A card still open when the day ends closes unrolled. That means the failure branch and no gold lost.
- **A failed Challenge costs gold only.** Nothing carries into a fight.

**Harder Challenges**

| Kind | DC | On a success | On a failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gathering Challenge | Weekday DC + 2 | +500g | −150g |
| Trial (announced before the roll) | Weekday DC + 4 | +750g | −150g |
| United Effort (§5) | Weekday DC | +300g to every friend, if the job is done | No gold lost |

- **A Gathering Challenge** is a Challenge played in the party chat on Day 7: the Approach Challenge. One friend steps up and rolls it in front of everyone (who rolls: §11). It is that friend's Daily Challenge.
- **A Trial** is a harder story Challenge. It takes the place of that day's Daily Challenge.

**The Challenge card,** shown after a MYTHIOS scene message:

```
THE MILLER'S WIFE · Difficulty Class 7

Charm +2 (Apprentice)
You Succeed On 6+ (Roll + 2 − DC 7)                    [ROLL]

Intellect +0 (Novice)
You Succeed On 8+ (Roll + 0 − DC 7)                    [ROLL]

Charm Relic · you hold 2
With +4, You Succeed On 2+ (used once, on a Charm roll)  [USE]

REWARD: +500g
IF YOU FAIL: −150g                                  [NOT NOW]
```

- **The card shows only the bonus a roll gets** (+2), never the Attribute's own number (38). That number is progress toward the next tier and lives on the Record, so it cannot be mistaken for something added to the roll (TVMen, 4 Oct 2026).
- **Each choice has its own button beside it** (4 Oct 2026): one [ROLL] for each Attribute, and [USE] beside the Relic. This replaces the old row of [ROLL CHARM] [ROLL INTELLECT] [USE RELIC] buttons under the card.
- A Challenge with one Attribute shows one choice and one [ROLL] button.
- The Relic choice is hidden when the player holds no matching Relic.

**The result,** followed by a MYTHIOS message with the story's success or failure branch:

```
CHALLENGE
You Roll 15 + 2 (Charm) − DC 7 = 10. SUCCESS

REWARD: +500g
```

```
CHALLENGE
You Roll 4 + 2 (Charm) − DC 7 = −1. FAIL

COST: −150g
```

## 4. Gear and fights

**Gear**
- Two tracks, Weapon and Armor, each with five levels. Both start at Level 1. They are bought separately.
- The gear table and its prices are in §12. Gold is the only gate.
- Each class has its own names for its weapon and armor. The numbers are the same for every class.

| Class | Weapon | Armor |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Longsword | Mail Coat |
| Artificer | Spring-Hammer | Riveted Apron |
| Sage | Ash Staff | Warded Cloak |
| Trickster | Sword-Cane | Leather Ward |
| Assassin | Paired Knives | Shadow Leathers |
| Archer | Yew Bow | Hunter's Jack |

On the Record and in the Store a piece of gear reads `Sword-Cane — Lv. 2 · Weapon 15`.

**A fight**
- Both sides have **100 HP**, at the start of every fight. Nothing carries from one fight to the next.
- Each round, the player attacks first, then the enemy.
- **Your attack hits when Roll + Weapon − DEF is 1 or more.** The enemy's attack hits when its Roll + ATK − your Armor is 1 or more. A tie misses.
- **A hit does damage equal to its margin × 5** (the Roll + Weapon − DEF result, times five). The same for both sides. *(Changed 14 Oct 2026, Fake Guru: keep the build's clear round line, e.g. "HIT +11 × 5 = 55. FOE HP 100 → 45". Was: a d20, doubled. Fight length and balance to be checked in the numbers pass.)*
- If your hit takes the enemy to 0 HP, the fight ends at once; the enemy does not strike back. Ties are impossible.
- A fight lasts about 4 to 6 rounds on average (re-simulated 15 Oct 2026 with margin × 5 damage; it was about 8 with a d20 × 2). It has no round limit; it ends when one side reaches 0 HP.
- **Each round is one tap** *(changed 14 Oct 2026, Fake Guru: keep the build's per-round tap, with a short pause after the tap to show the roll; this replaces the 4 Oct rule below)*. Earlier rule: **a fight rolls itself, one beat at a time** (decided 4 Oct 2026). The player taps [FIGHT] once. Each round then fills in line by line in one message: the player's die tumbles for about a second and lands; on a hit the damage die does the same; then the enemy's. There is no button to roll each blow and no setting for it. A round is never shown all at once.

**Enemies**
- **Each Gathering has one kind of enemy, the same for every friend,** with a fixed reward. Each friend present fights one of them. The table is in §12.
- **A weekday enemy** (the Episode 1 tutorial and every weekday fight) is one lower in DEF and one lower in ATK than that episode's Gathering enemy.
- **Fight gold (set 5 Oct 2026, Audit G).** A weekday fight on a day with no Challenge pays 300g on a win and nothing on a loss (the same as the tutorial). A weekday fight on a day that also has a Challenge pays nothing: the Challenge is that day's paid beat. A Gathering Battle pays the episode's reward (§12).

**The enemy card,** one chat message from MYTHIOS:

```
MINE GUARD · DEF 23 · ATK 16
YOU · Weapon 14 · Armor 27

You Hit On 10+ (Roll + Weapon 14 − DEF 23)
Enemy Hits On 12+ (Roll + ATK 16 − Armor 27)

Damage = Margin × 5
REWARD: 380g

[FIGHT]  [NOT NOW]
```

- The example shows the Episode 1 Gathering enemy's numbers.
- No HP lines and no percentages. HP is always 100.
- **Buttons depend on the fight.** A weekday fight shows [FIGHT] only. A Gathering Battle shows no buttons: the card is followed by "The fight begins in 10 seconds".

**A round,** one message in the player's own thread, capitalised as shown:

```
Round 1
You Roll 14 + Weapon 14 − DEF 23 = 5. HIT
Damage = 5 × 5 = 25. Guard HP 100 → 75
Enemy Rolls 7 + ATK 16 − Armor 27 = −4. MISS
```

- A hit is followed by a line with the damage and the target's HP before and after, with no indent and no mark in front of it. A miss has no second line.
- **The Challenge line shows the score and its bonus** *(14 Oct 2026, Fake Guru)*: "🎲 13 + Charm 38 (+2) − DC 7 = +8". The score is shown so the number the player grows is visible; only the tier bonus counts.
- **Words and numbers are in different colours** (numbers in the gold standard, `mythios-visual-style-guide.md`).

**The result**

```
VICTORY. Mine Guard defeated in 6 rounds.
REWARD: +380g
```

```
DEFEATED.
REWARD: none
```

Inside a Gathering Battle a loss reads `DEFEATED. You are down for this Battle. Your friends fight on.`

**Nobody dies.** A defeated player is down, not dead. Straight after a lost fight MYTHIOS says in the story what happened to them, and the story goes on. This is never announced before a fight.

**The Gathering Battle (2 to 6 friends)**
- Each friend fights their own enemy, one to one.
- All fights run in lockstep: Round 1 for everyone, a shared 10-second pause, then Round 2.
- **Help-call:** when a friend's HP drops below 50, a message appears in the party chat and in every friend's own thread: `MEI IS IN TROUBLE · HP 42` with a [GIVE A COMBAT RELIC] button. The first friend to tap gives one (§5); a second tap does nothing. The call goes out once for each friend in a Battle, straight after the round in which their HP first fell below 50, and closes when the next round starts. If nobody gives one, nothing happens. If several friends are below 50, the call goes out first for the one with the lowest HP.
- **Reinforcement:** a friend who has won chooses whom to fight beside (decided 4 Oct 2026). A card lists each friend still fighting with their HP and their enemy's HP, each with a [JOIN] button. They join the fight that most needs it at once (the friend with the lowest HP, or a fallen friend's enemy), so no round is lost, and may switch to another friend with a tap. Their attack appears as its own line each round in that fight's thread. They hit with their own Weapon and cannot be hurt. If two fights are equally in need, they join the friend who comes first in the party list. A fallen friend's enemy stops attacking.
- **The party wins when every enemy is down.** A friend who falls does not end the Battle.
- **If every friend falls,** the Battle is lost. The enemy steps over the party and leaves them alive, nobody is paid, and the scene carries on as written.
- **Fighting beside a friend is free.** Reinforcing and arriving late cost nothing. Only help with a roll costs a Relic (§5).
- **A fallen friend** watches and cannot act for the rest of that Battle.
- **Every friend present is paid the Battle's reward when the party wins,** whoever landed the last blow.

## 5. Relics and helping a friend

**Relics**
- Seven types: one **Combat Relic** and six **Attribute Relics,** one for each Attribute.
- Each is used once. The price is in §12. A Relic cannot be bought while your own card or fight is open. There is no limit on how many a player holds.
- Relics can be given to a friend. Gold cannot.
- **An Attribute Relic** gives +4 on one Challenge that uses its Attribute. One Relic per Challenge.
- **A Combat Relic** counts as one extra gear level (+1 Weapon and +1 Armor) for the rest of one fight. A fighter can benefit from only one in a fight, their own or a friend's. A [USE RELIC] button sits under each round's message; a Relic used or given after a round takes effect from the next round.

**Helping a friend always costs a Relic.** There is no free help.
- **On a weekday:** give a Relic to a friend from your Relic Box. They use it on their own card.
- **In a fight:** answer a help-call by giving a Combat Relic. From the next round the helped friend's lines carry +1 Weapon and +1 Armor, marked with the giver's name.
- **In a United Effort:** see below.

**United Effort**
*(Rewritten 4 Oct 2026, approved by TVMen. The old rule, one hard roll by one friend helped by Relics, is retired: §14.)*

- **One job the whole party does together, told as a story in the party chat.** Every Gathering has one. The six jobs, with every line, are in `mythios-united-effort-stories.md`.
- **Two things each start at 100:** the thing in the way, and the thing the party stands to lose. Each is named by the scene (The Pin · His Breath). Where the thing at risk is a friend, it is that friend's HP.
- **One friend at a time rolls an ordinary Challenge** in the night's Attribute, against the episode's **weekday DC**, on the usual Challenge card.
- **A SUCCESS rolls damage against the thing in the way. A FAIL rolls damage against the thing the party stands to lose.** Damage is 10 + a d20.
- After every roll MYTHIOS posts the result, the damage, one line of story, and the hand-on.
- **It ends when either thing reaches 0.** If the thing in the way reaches 0, the job is done. Both endings lead to the same next scene.
- **Who goes next is the party's choice.** MYTHIOS names the first friend. After that the friend who has just rolled hands it on with a button for each friend who has not yet gone. Nobody goes twice until everyone has gone once. If no choice is made in ten seconds, MYTHIOS names the next.
- **Relics:** the roller may use their own matching Attribute Relic, or any one friend may give the roller any Attribute Relic. One Relic to a roll, for +4.
- **A friend who fell in the Battle still takes their turn.**
- **A friend alone** takes every turn, and both things start at 60.
- **Reward:** 300g to every friend present if the job is done. Nothing if it is lost. A failed roll costs no gold. Relics used are spent either way. No Attribute grows from these rolls.
- **The story lines:** each is chosen by where the damaged thing now stands (above two-thirds, the middle third, the last third, or 0). Each band has two lines; no line is said twice. A line speaks only of its own thing. In a FAIL line the place does the harm, never the friend who rolled.
- The Attribute is fixed for each episode (§12) and named in that day's "Tonight" message (§11).

## 6. Seeker Fragments and gold

**Seeker Fragments**
- One fragment arrives each weekday. **It is in every friend's Seeker Chest from the moment the day opens,** whether or not they have read the beat.
- **The fragment is its own puzzle.** It shows its own text with two words missing and their letters jumbled. The words are listed in `mythios-fragment-registry.md`.
- Each missing word is its own lock, worth **25g,** and shows the solver's name once filled.
- **Until the fragment's own day ends, a player may fill only one of its two words.** After that, anyone may fill what is left, and it still pays 25g a word. If two friends send the same word at the same moment, the first to arrive gets it.
- A friend who finds a fragment already open can still unjumble its words for fun, for no gold and no credit.
- **Solving happens inside the Seeker Chest:** tap the letters in order, or type the word. A wrong try costs nothing.
- When a fragment opens, its words are open for the whole party, and a line goes to the party chat.
- **At the end of Day 6** anything unsolved completes itself, with no gold and no name on it, and the six fragments form the week's map. The map is told in words: MYTHIOS posts the map scene from the script, and the Seeker Chest shows the six fragments together under one line naming where they point.

**Gold**
- Gold never goes below 0. A cost a player cannot fully pay takes them to 0, and nothing more is owed.
- Starting gold is 0.

| Gold comes from | Amount |
|---|---|
| A Daily Challenge | +500g on a success, −150g on a failure |
| A Trial | +750g, −150g |
| A fragment word | +25g |
| The tutorial fight, and a weekday fight on a day with no Challenge (a win) | +300g |
| A [PRESS ON], passed | +200g |
| [LOOK CLOSER], passed | +75g |
| Match, each right pick (doubled if every friend present is right) | +50g (+100g) |
| A Gathering Battle | The episode's reward (§12) |
| The Approach Challenge | +500g, −150g |
| A United Effort | +300g to every friend, if the job is done |

Gold is spent on gear and Relics (§12).

## 7. Session Zero

Told by MYTHIOS in the player's own thread before Day 1 opens. About three minutes.

**Step 1 — The premise**

> **Tales of the Dark Moon.**
>
> In the hill villages, children are going missing, and nobody is looking for them.
>
> Before the week is out, it will be your business.
>
> One short chapter a day. One night a week, you meet the others.
>
> **[BEGIN]**

**Step 2 — Who you are**

> Choose the one who will walk this road.

| Class | Attribute | The line on the card |
|---|---|---|
| **Warrior** | Might | When a door won't open, you are the reason it does. |
| **Artificer** | Craft | You know how things are put together, and so how they come apart. |
| **Sage** | Intellect | You notice what the room is not saying. |
| **Trickster** | Charm | People tell you things. They are not always sure why. |
| **Assassin** | Agility | You are already where they weren't looking. |
| **Archer** | Aim | You see the one thing that matters, from a long way off. |

- Each card says plainly, with its own Attribute: *"Your best Attribute is Might. The road will ask for all six."*
- If a friend has already taken a class: *"Mei is already a Sage. You can be one too. The road will ask for all six."*

**Step 3 — A second strength**

> Nobody is only one thing. What else are you good at?

The player picks one of the other five Attributes.

**Step 4 — Two questions, both optional**

> Where were you born?
>
> What was a grandparent's name?
>
> *You can skip these. They only make the story a little more yours.*

These feed the personal stake beside the player's name on Meira's list.

**Step 5 — Your starting card**

```
YOUR CHARACTER
Trickster

Charm 14 · Agility 12
Might 10 · Craft 10 · Intellect 10 · Aim 10
All Novice (+0 on a Challenge)

Weapon 14 (Level 1) · Armor 27 (Level 1)
HP 100
Gold 0

[READY]
```

The card says "Your character", never "Your Seeker": a player does not learn what a Seeker is until the story tells them. The words "Seeker Fragment" and "Seeker Chest" do appear from Day 1, as a name the player cannot yet explain. That is intended.

Class and second Attribute cannot be changed once Day 1 opens.

**Step 6 — The door**

> Day 1 opens at 5:00am. Sleep, if you can.

**First-time lines.** Each is shown once, in MYTHIOS's plain voice, at the moment the thing first appears. They are not story and not Garrick's.

| When it appears | The line |

## 11. How a session runs

**The clock**
- **One party clock.** The host picks the party's time zone. A day opens at 5:00am party time and ends at 5:00am the next day. Every rule that says "when the day ends" uses this. Friends elsewhere see times in their own zone.

**Starting a season**
- The host creates the party, picks the time zone and sends an invite link.
- **The host decides when Episode 1 starts.** That day is Day 1, and each Gathering falls six days after its episode begins.
- Each friend completes Session Zero before Day 1 opens.
- A friend may still join until the end of Episode 1 Day 2. After that the party is fixed for the season.
- **A friend who joins late** does Session Zero on joining, then plays Day 1 in full (it is a solo day: the fight, the Challenge, the list and the fragment, with the same gold), and then the current day. Both days' story Challenges are Daily Challenges for them.
- If a friend leaves, the party plays on, even with one, and later scenes use the new [N]. A friend who has left cannot rejoin this season.
- Once Episode 1 has started the clock does not pause. A day nobody plays is a missed day.

**The Gathering's hour**
- On Day 4 MYTHIOS posts a poll in the party chat with a few time slots for Day 7. Friends tap every slot they can make.
- At the end of Day 5 the host picks the time and pins it. If the host does not, the app decides: the slot most friends can make, or last week's time if nobody voted.
- The host can still change it until the lobby opens; everyone is told of the change. The Gathering stays on Day 7.
- Everyone gets a reminder an hour before.

**Day 7 before the Gathering**
- When the day opens, each thread gets a short "Tonight" message: where the party stands, when the Gathering starts, and which Attribute tonight's United Effort will call on. For example: *"Tonight: the Gathering, 8:00pm. The map points under the hill. Tonight calls on Intellect."* MYTHIOS writes it from Day 6's Pull.
- There is no chapter or fragment on Day 7. Talk with Garrick is open as always.

**Starting the Gathering**
- At the agreed time a lobby opens in the party chat showing who is here.
- If everyone who said they could come is in, it starts at once.
- After 10 minutes, any friend present can tap [START]. After 30 minutes it starts by itself.
- The host can start it at any time.
- It runs with whoever is there, even one friend alone, with one enemy per friend present.
- If nobody comes before Day 7 ends, everyone gets the recap in the morning and the story moves on. No Challenge is rolled and no gold is paid.

**Late, absent, or dropped**
- **Arriving during the Battle:** the friend joins from the next round as a reinforcer (§4), attacking whichever fight needs it most, and is paid the Battle's reward if the party wins. This is intended, however late they arrive.
- **Arriving after the Battle:** the friend joins from the next act and gets no Battle gold.
- Either way they get one "what you missed" line on joining.
- **Dropping out mid-Battle:** rolls are automatic, so the fight carries on and the result, and the gold if the party wins, is waiting.
- **Present but silent:** a friend in the lobby when the Gathering starts has an enemy and is paid like anyone else.
- **Missing the whole Gathering:** the friend gets the recap. There is no catch-up fight and no Battle gold.

**The Gathering toolkit (decided 4 Oct 2026; details in `mythios-gathering-plan.md`)**
- **The Approach vote shows who is best at each option** (decided 4 Oct): beside each option, its Attribute and the name and score of the party's strongest friend in it.
- **The Approach decides who strikes first** in the Battle: the party on a pass, the enemy on a failure. This supersedes the 3 Oct note that a failed roll carries no "strikes first" cost.
- **Each Battle may carry one condition,** stated in one line on the fight card. The conditions and their numbers are in §12.
- **Notice:** after the Battle each friend gets one private line keyed to their highest Attribute, with an optional `[LOOK CLOSER]` roll on that Attribute. A friend who fell gets a "from the floor" line (still a true detail worth telling) and no roll.
- **The United Effort's result changes something the party can see** for the rest of the night, never what is revealed. (One exception, decided 4 Oct: in Gathering 4 a lost job means no captive and no Ask board. It rides on the whole party's rolls, not on one.)
- **One result carries into a weekday:** a lost United Effort in Gathering 5 makes Week 6 Day 1's Challenge two harder.
- **Nothing at a Gathering is a race.** Where one friend must be chosen for a big moment, MYTHIOS picks at random from those who have not rolled tonight.
- **Garrick never volunteers.** A question to him that nobody taps goes unasked.
- **Match:** MYTHIOS posts a MATCH card in the party chat with a line of scripture and the week's six fragments as rows (day and place found, first words; tap a row to read the whole fragment in the card) (the place labels are in `mythios-match-labels.md`), each with a private [PICK]. The player never goes to the Bag. A small line shows who has picked, never what. When every friend present has picked, or after sixty seconds (set 5 Oct; was twenty), the right row lights up with the initials of those who matched it. **A wrong pick is never shown** (no number records a miss). The result reads "Mei and Jo matched it." (or "All of you matched it.") Every right pick is paid and named together (never who was first), a wrong pick costs nothing, and a friend who has not picked is simply not counted. **Each right pick pays 50g, or 100g if every friend present is right (set 5 Oct, Audit G).** At most two a night. Gathering 1 has none.
- **Ask:** a board of questions for a character who must answer; one per friend (two each in a party of two, four alone). Questions the night needs are marked and cannot be missed: after two quiet minutes their answers are given. Other answers can be missed. On the phone the board is grouped, sealed questions first. A board may stay up for the rest of the night and gain questions as things are found; each friend still spends one. At most one board a night.
- **No friend speaks.** No script puts a spoken line, or a scripted act, in a friend's mouth or hands. This replaces the older allowance that "one of you says" may stay. A line becomes a question to the table, a button a friend may tap, or narration of what everyone sees.
- **Show and stop:** for a night's biggest line, MYTHIOS posts the evidence and waits; it does not explain a twist after it lands.
- **Rolls at a Gathering:** one friend rolls the Approach. Friends take turns at the United Effort (§5) until the job ends; a friend who never rolls still gets the 300g if it is won. `[LOOK CLOSER]` is separate and open to every friend.

**Pacing and choices in the Gathering**
- MYTHIOS posts the story a short passage at a time. The next follows after a few seconds, or at once when any friend taps [GO ON].
- **A found document is read by the party, not to it.** Each paragraph is its own message, and the next comes only when a friend taps [READ ON]. No message is longer than about 120 words.
- Where the script expects questions, the story holds until a friend taps [GO ON], or for two quiet minutes.
- **A question put to the table** holds until a friend answers: one nudge after sixty quiet seconds, the answer after ninety. MYTHIOS never grades what a friend says.
- **A friend is named only for what they really did** (the one who rolled, the one who tapped). A small act the story wants from a friend is a button.
- A Gathering takes about 20 to 30 minutes with the table talking. The script alone, played by one friend with no chat, timed at 15 minutes typical and up to 24 with slow waits (paper timing, 5 Oct 2026; was "30 to 45", which nothing in the script supports). A real play was skipped on 5 Oct 2026 (TV); the paper timing stands.
- **The group Choice:** each friend present taps one option. The majority wins after 30 seconds, or sooner once everyone has tapped. A tie goes to the first vote cast.
- **Who rolls the Approach:** if several step up, it goes to whoever has rolled fewest Approaches this season; a tie goes to the first tap. If nobody steps up within 30 seconds, it goes to whoever has rolled fewest. (The United Effort is rolled by everyone in turn: §5.)
- **A Choice a script gives to one friend** (as in Episode 6) is that friend's alone and is not put to a vote.

**Recap**
- A player who opens their thread after missing days first gets a short recap of each missed day, then today's beat.
- No more than five lines for a missed day and fifteen for a missed Gathering. Every `[PLANT]` line is kept. A day's recap ends on that day's Pull.
- A recap tells the plot both branches share. It does not tell a missed Challenge as a failure.
- A Gathering's recap always states that Gathering's Twist in plain words, whether or not the line is marked `[PLANT]`.
- Missed days' Challenges, gold and growth are gone. Missed fragments are in the Seeker Chest, solved or still open.

**The GM's limits**
- **The road always goes on.** A player may try anything and the GM answers in the story, but each day still ends at that day's Pull.
- **Scripted characters cannot be killed, captured or driven off in Talk:** Garrick, the children, and anyone a later scene needs. An attack on one is turned aside in the story and never becomes a fight card.
- **Plot lives only in the scripted beats.** In Talk no character reveals anything a later beat is meant to deliver. Garrick, who may never deny knowledge, uses his scripted deflections. Asked something no script covers, he answers with small talk about the road, the food or the weather, or turns the question back, and never states a fact about the plot.
- **A player's thread is private.** MYTHIOS never tells one friend what another chose, rolled, guessed or asked in their own thread, beyond the one result line.
- **Talk stays in today's place with today's people.** Anything the GM says there is small and disposable, and never reuses a name or object that matters in the canon.
- **Tone:** the GM steers away from cruelty, above all toward the children.
- **Real names:** where a script credits a deed ("whoever found the week's first fragment"), MYTHIOS names the real friend when its records say who that is. It never puts a spoken line in a named friend's mouth: "one of you says" stays "one of you".

**What the GM remembers**
- **For each player:** class, Attributes, gear, gold and Relics; each day's Challenge result and branch; Choices made; notes; fragment words solved; every exchange with Garrick and every theory answer; days played and Gatherings attended.
- **For the party:** each Gathering's Choice and roll results, the Episode 6 choice, the weekly poll.
- **Tomorrow's beat** is the written text. It changes only in marked slots: the branch line a player earned, real names, [N], the class's gear names. MYTHIOS never rewrites a beat, and never builds one from a player's theory answer or chat.

**The end of a season**
- After the last Gathering each friend gets a season summary (their stats and notes), and the Garrick Recap unlocks (`mythios-garrick-recap.md`).
- The summary ends with: **"Your Seeker's story continues."**

## 12a. Rules for the builder *(added 2026-10-03 after Audit E)*

These settle cases the rules above left open. The engineering notes are in `mythios-build-notes.md`.

**Dice and records**
- **The server rolls every die.** A tap only means "go". The phone never decides a result.
- Gold, Relics, rolls, growth and fragment words are saved the instant they change, and each change is applied once only, however many times a message is sent.

**The day**
- **A weekday counts as played** when that friend's beat has been delivered to its last line. Solving a fragment alone does not count. **Day 7 counts as played** for a friend who was in the Gathering at any point.
- **A card left unrolled when the day ends** gives the failure branch, costs no gold.
- **The tutorial fight** has no [NOT NOW]. If it is unfought when the day ends, it runs by itself and pays its real result.
- **Anything open when the day ends** is finished by itself: a fight is rolled out, a set-aside card expires.
- **The result line in the party chat:** a pass reads "TV passed today's Challenge."; a rolled failure reads "TV failed today's Challenge."; a day with no Challenge reads "TV has read today's chapter."; an expired card posts nothing. It never names the Attribute or the branch.
- **After missed days** the recap ends with one plain line: "You missed two days. The story went on. Today is still yours."

**Cards and Relics**
- **[USE] on a Challenge** arms the +4 on the matching Attribute and changes that line of the card. A second tap takes it back. The Relic is spent only when the roll is made. If both Attributes have a Relic, each has its own button.
- **A Combat Relic** helps a fighter once per fight. While one is active the button is hidden and a friend's offer is turned away.

**The party**
- **The party list** is the host, then the friends in the order they finished Session Zero. It never changes order. Every tie the rules send to "the party list" uses it.
- **A season cannot start** until at least two friends have tapped [READY].
- **A friend who joins late** plays Day 1 and then the current day, in that order. They count as two days played (12, then 9). On Day 1's fragment they may still claim an unfilled word.

**The season's clock**
- **The server keeps the time.** A phone's own clock is ignored.
- **The party's time zone is locked** when Episode 1 starts.
- **A day opens at 5:00am on the party's wall clock.** When the clocks change, one day is an hour shorter or longer. A Gathering already scheduled keeps its wall-clock time.
- **A season started after 5:00am** opens Day 1 at the next 5:00am.
- **Episodes run back to back.** The 5:00am that ends Day 7 opens the next episode's Day 1. A friend who missed the Gathering gets its recap first, then the new beat.
- **The poll offers only times from 8:00am to 11:00pm.** A Gathering still running at 5:00am carries on to its end; the next day's beat waits for it.

**The Gathering**
- **"Here" means** a friend has the Gathering open when it starts, or opens it later.
- **It starts once.** The first of the four start conditions to happen starts it; the others then do nothing.
- **When nobody steps up to roll,** the roll goes to the friend who has not yet rolled tonight and has rolled fewest at Gatherings this season, then by the party list.
- **Shared buttons** ([GO ON], [READ ON], the group Choice, a help-call): the first tap to reach the server counts, and the rest do nothing.
- **A Gathering nobody attends:** the recap tells the story as the script's success path, with its Twist. No Challenge is rolled, and nobody earns gold or growth.
- **The story never waits on the AI.** Every scripted line is stored and sent by the server. If MYTHIOS is slow or fails, the scripted text still arrives.

**Fragments**
- A fragment is **in the Chest** from the day's opening, **part-solved** when one word is filled, and **complete** when both are. Each word filled posts one line in the party chat, naming the friend.
- The server checks each try and never sends the answer to the phone. After five wrong tries at one word, that friend waits two minutes.

**People and accounts** *(TVMen, 2026-10-03)*
- **Sign-in:** with a Google account, an Apple account, or an email address and a password.
- **No age check.** The app does not ask a player's age (TVMen, 2026-10-03).
- **The party has a name,** chosen by the host when they make it.
- **Joining:** the invite link asks to join; the host approves each friend. One link per party; it works until the party has six or until the end of Episode 1 Day 2. The host can make a new link, and the old one stops working.
- **The invite page** is headed "Join the adventure in Tales of the Dark Moon", names who sent it and the party, and says what it asks: six weeks, about ten minutes a day, one evening a week, free (changed from "about five minutes" by Fake Guru, 16 Oct: the day now takes 8 to 12 minutes); and carries the credit line "A fake guru game. The story is told by Mythios AI."
- **Mythios, series and seasons** (Fake Guru, 16 Oct 2026). The app is **Mythios**. "Tales of the Dark Moon" is one **series** of many, and a series is told in **seasons**; a party plays one season of one series.
- **More than one party:** a person may be in several parties at once. *(Changed 16 Oct 2026: they play the same character in every party of the same series; see "Two names".)*
- **Name lengths:** a player's name is 1 to 12 characters; a party's name is 1 to 20. Both fit on one line of the Record and the chat.
- **Two names** (decided 4 Oct 2026; changed by Fake Guru 16 Oct 2026). An account has one name. In each **series** a person plays one **character**, with its own **character name**, so a person may be "Jennifer" in Tales of the Dark Moon and someone else in another series. The character (its name, class, second strength and Session Zero answers) is made in their first Session Zero of that series and kept for every party and season of it. The character name is the one shown on the Card and in the party's chat; changing it changes it in every party of that series. *(Was: a separate character name for each party.)*
- **A player's name** can be changed at any time; the party chat shows the change once ("Dev is now Devan.").
- **Leaving:** a friend who leaves, or is removed, cannot come back to that story. The party chat says "Dev has left the road." and the story does not remark on it.
- **Reporting:** press and hold a message in the party chat, then [REPORT]. The report comes to the makers; the person reported is not told.
- The screens and their wording are in `review-accounts-and-invites.md`.
- **Removing:** the host can remove a friend at any time. The party plays on, as when a friend leaves.
- **A new host:** if the host leaves, the friend who joined first becomes host. The party chat says "Mei now leads the party."
- **Deleting an account:** the player's data is deleted. Their character stays in the party's story as a name only.
- **After the season,** each character is kept, for a second season.
- **The game is free** for now.

**If the service itself is down**
- If the app is out of action for most of a day, through no fault of the players, the season's clock freezes and picks up where it stopped. No day is lost. (This is different from a day nobody plays: that is a missed day, and the story goes on.)

**Garrick**
- **Each weekday he has one scripted line** in the party chat, posted when the day opens. Anything more is small talk about the road, the food and the weather.
- **Any question that touches** himself, Meira, the Order, the Stones, the king, the children or what happens next is answered only from the deception table's rows for that episode, or with a stored deflection. The AI does not write these answers. This is enforced by the app, not left to the AI's judgement.
- **There is no out-of-game conversation (decided 5 Oct 2026, TV).** A question about how the game works, about being an AI, who made it, or anything in the real world is answered in the story, by a stored line that stays in the world and never denies anything. It points at the game's own things ("The Store keeps its own prices.", "Your Record keeps that count.") or turns the question aside ("I'm Garrick. I keep an inn."). Garrick never claims to be a person and never says he does not know what an AI is. The AI never writes these lines. Who made it and the fact that the story is told by an AI are said outside the chat only, on the invite page and in Settings, under About: "A fake guru game. The story is told by Mythios AI."
- **The ledger's [ASK HIM]:** the first tap by any friend posts his scripted line once in the party chat and opens the ledger for everyone. Later taps only open it.
