#### Arrival

> The map was right. Deeper in the hill than the working chamber where they drove you back, down a shaft of its own, there is a stair. It was cut so long ago that its edges have worn soft as soap. The air on it is cold, and has not been breathed in a long time.
>
> At its foot is a second room, older than the one above it, older than the mill, older than anyone's memory of this valley.
>
> You hear the chanting before you see the light. Low, even, patient: the same few words, over and over, too muffled to make out. It is not the sound of people in a hurry. It is the sound of people who have done this many times before.

#### The black circle

> Somebody lifts the book out from under the fallen lamp and beats the sparks out of its corner. It is small, bound in black cloth, and still warm from the flame. The cloth is worn shiny in one place, where a thumb has held it a great many times.

> This belongs to *them*: their scripture, the kind of thing a man carries against his chest. On its first page, under a black circle painted like a moon with no light in it, is a title: *The First Book of the Order of the Dark Moon.*

#### The United Effort

> You have the black book in your hands when the far wall begins to sound.
>
> It comes from the hole they dug: boots, a great many, and lamplight growing in it like water rising in a pipe. They left with what they came for. Now they are coming back for what they left behind: their dead, and their book.
>
> There are more lamps in that tunnel than you can count. This is not a fight.
>
> But they cut that tunnel in a night and a day, through ground nobody had disturbed in a thousand years, and it is standing on green timber and hope. Take out the right prop, and the hill will shut it for you. Take out the wrong one, and the fall comes down on your side of it.
>
> Every fall changes what is holding what. So one of you looks, and calls it, and the nearest boot does the rest. Then the next of you looks again.

#### The breach

*⚙ Either way, the whole party reads this:*

Each turn is a Intellect Challenge against DC 7. Pass: 10 + d20 off the thing in the way. Fail: 10 + d20 off the thing at risk. It ends when either bar hits 0. Won: +300g to every friend present. - **On a pass:**

  - *(early)* The cross-piece. It was carrying nothing until the first fall; now it is carrying everything. Out it comes. The lamplight in the hole narrows to a slot.
  - *(middle)* Left-hand post, low down. The roof behind it comes down in a long soft rush, and the boots on the far side stop.
  - *(middle)* You call it, and you are right. Somewhere in the dust, on their side, a man is coughing.
  - *(late)* One frame is holding all of it now. You can see which leg.
  - *(late)* The slot is a hand wide. A lamp is pushed up to it from the other side, and an eye.
  - *(the end)* The last leg goes. The hill shuts like a book. The dust takes a long time to settle, and when it has there is no hole, no light, and no sound from the other side at all.
- **On a fail:**
  - *(early)* The big prop, the obvious one. It was holding up your side. A slab of the old wall leans out and comes down across the floor, and one of the lamps is under it.

  - *(middle)* The fall comes your way. When the dust thins, the far end of the room is rubble, and the stone table is the edge of what is left.
  - *(middle)* A crack runs across the ceiling from the hole to the stair, as quick as a lizard.
  - *(late)* There is room to stand, and not much more. Everything you have not got in your hands is under stone.
  - *(late)* The foot of the stair is going.
  - *(the end)* The room comes in.
- **Between turns:** Corin has put both hands over his ears. / On the stone table, the lamps the fight kicked over are guttering. / Whoever is coming is not hurrying. They have never needed to.
