How a lead works: the button shows the **place**. The player picks one of its two **rolls**. The **clue always comes back**. A pass also gives the first-letter **hint** for that fragment word. A fail gives the clue with a **late line** and no hint. **Leans** is for checking only and is never shown.

Each day has one lead leaning A, one leaning B, and one that complicates both. Two leads hint word 1, one hints word 2.

## Doubts for Fake Guru

1. **Day 4's canon clue (the leaned bar)** is at the gate where Garrick is later standing ("already holding the gate at the bottom of the lane"). A player could wonder whether he leaned the bar. Canon says the lookout went through first, so it is not false, but it sits close to him. Keep, or move it?
2. **Day 5's "look" choice** (the step, the fire, the path) could give leads too, as the proposal suggests for "look" days. I did not use them: the player has already read one of the three, so it would not be new to them.
3. **Day 1 is played alone** with no board, so its three leads matter only to the player's own fire and week-board line.
