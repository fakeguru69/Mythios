# Campfire nudges: rewrite draft (DRAFT, 15 Oct 2026, not approved)

**Status: DRAFT for Fake Guru. Nothing in the game has changed.** If approved, the new lines go into `docs/canon/mythios-campfire-table-v2.md` first (it is the Campfire master), then `src/data/campfire-days-v23.json` and `src/data/campfireDaysData.ts` (both hold a copy of each nudge).

**What a nudge is.** Garrick's one question to one friend at the fire, stored as `{friend:Attribute}, question?`. The game puts in the name of the friend at the fire with the highest score in that Attribute, skipping yesterday's friend (Campfire §M). With one friend at the fire, the name is dropped and the question is asked straight to them.

**Why rewrite.** The audit (`docs/audits/point-6-campfire-discussions.md` §A4 and Part B) found most nudges are practical ("how fast could we reach the mill?") instead of pushing the mystery. Qwen also flagged wrong Attributes on W1D5, W2D1, W3D1, W5D4 and W6D5.

**My count:** 23 practical, 13 investigative. The audit said 21 and 15; the difference is two borderline ones (3-2 "how do you ask a village what it saw?" and 4-1 "where in that room would you stand?"), which I count as practical because they ask *how to act*, not *what it means*.

**Rules every proposed line follows**
- Tied to that day's fork (forkA / forkB) or to something every player learned that day (the day's story or opener), so it works whichever Fan Out lead a friend picked.
- Under 15 words. The Attribute fits the question.
- Garrick's voice: warm, plain, a question only. No promise, no prediction, no "maybe / perhaps / possibly", no sarcasm. Never points at Garrick, the Order's secrets, whose blood, or the finale.
- First-use gates kept: no "Order", "four", "king", "five", "moon-blood", "Highwall" or "heartbeat" in any proposed line. Names used (Corin, Wren, Joss, Meira) are all known by that day.
- No new lore: every fact named is in that day's episode text or Campfire entry.

## W1D1 (no Garrick)

- **How it works now:** W1D1 is narrated, with no Garrick, and canon says the day is played alone (the friends meet on W1D2). But the fire screen starts with **the whole party seated**, so the line names the friend with the highest Intellect (Mei in the sample party), someone the player has not met yet. The label reads "ROADSIDE NUDGE · INTELLECT".
- **What to do:** keep the line. It is investigative and fits the fork. Fix the seating, not the text: on W1D1 (the one night with `narratedNoGarrick`) the fire should seat only the player. The game already drops the name when one friend is at the fire, so the narrator asks: *"What is odd about a stranger who leaves something behind?"* That is a code change in `CampfireEveningView.tsx` (who is at the fire), not a text change. I did not make it.
- **Side note:** on every other night the label shows the rule to the player ("GARRICK NUDGE · HIGHEST CRAFT (Mei)"). Showing the friend's name is fine; "HIGHEST CRAFT" shows how the game works. Worth a look in the interface pass.

## Doubts (3)

1. **4-4 sits next to "whose blood".** The fork itself asks what "moon-blood" means. My line asks only why Meira chose the old word. It does not say "blood" and points at no one. If even that is too close, the safe swap is **{friend:Intellect}, a whole street under water, and its windows still have lamp-hooks. Why keep them?** It uses a Fan Out lead ("The old hooks"), so only friends who picked that lead would know it.
2. **3-3 keeps a question about something the player never sees.** "A number she doubted" depends on the W3D3 plant that sits only in the hidden note (audit A3). It is investigative, so I left it, but it will puzzle players until the plant is shown in the day. If you would rather replace it now: **{friend:Intellect}, were these children picked for where they live, or the places for them?** (the fork itself; "five" is allowed from W3D3 but not needed).
3. **6-5 and 6-6 are one and two nights before the finale.** Both lines stay with what the day showed and do not steer the door choice. But anything Garrick asks in Week 6 reads differently once players know who he is. Please read both with that in mind. I avoided "Is that door ours to open?" for 6-6 on purpose: from him, that would be steering.
