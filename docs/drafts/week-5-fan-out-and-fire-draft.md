How a lead works: the button shows the **place**. The player picks one of its two **rolls**. The **clue always comes back**. A pass also gives the first-letter **hint** for that fragment word. A fail gives the clue with a **late line** and no hint. **Leans** is for checking only and is never shown.

Each day has one lead leaning A, one leaning B, and one that complicates both. Two leads hint word 1, one hints word 2.

## Week 5 question (NEW; the case so far)

**What will it take for the fifth to end this?**

*Fits the episode's investigation line: "The torn page says a fifth Stone can end it. Meira never found a fifth." The "where" is answered on Day 6 (in the wall), so the week question keeps only the "what it takes", which stays open until the Gathering (their blood does nothing; "where he fell, under the dark moon"). It stays true to the end: in the finale the fifth does end the sleep, only not the way the party hopes. It never asks whose blood.*

## Doubts for Fake Guru

1. **Day 1 "Every window lit"** points at the dark moon a day early. It is canon custom and true, and the Gathering's answer ("under the dark moon") is the players' to find, so I kept it. If it gives too much, swap it for a plainer "both" lead.
2. **Day 4 "Metal on stone"** is the strongest B lead in the week. Canon wants "Nobody is sure", and one sound keeps that, but it leans hard. Keep, or soften to "a sound you cannot place"?
3. **Day 5's fork** is already one-sided (they count to the dark moon, a date). The B lead (heads lift at the beat) is true but weaker than the A lead. A fork rewrite (proposal §3.7) may suit this day better than a stronger B clue.
