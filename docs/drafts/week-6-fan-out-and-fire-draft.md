How a lead works: the button shows the **place**. The player picks one of its two **rolls**. The **clue always comes back**. A pass also gives the first-letter **hint** for that fragment word. A fail gives the clue with a **late line** and no hint. **Leans** is for checking only and is never shown.

Each day has one lead leaning A, one leaning B, and one that complicates both. Two leads hint word 1, one hints word 2.

Week 6 rules on top of Week 1's: Garrick is with the party every day, so no lead sits at his place (the top step on Day 5, the sill on Day 6) and no clue is about him. Nothing may point at the trapdoor his household ran by, or at whose blood wakes the fifth. No place label or clue uses that day's two fragment words.

## Doubts for Fake Guru

1. **Day 3's B lead (clean robes)** leans B only lightly: robes say the new men expect to be somewhere for a rite, not that they have a way in. A stronger B clue would have to hint at another door, and canon keeps that for Day 4 ("There is another door. And the Order has it."). Keep it light, or want it stronger?
2. **Day 4's "Louder here" and Day 6's "The swept fan"** both sit close to the blank rock being a door. Day 6 reveals it as a door before the Fan Out, so the fan is safe. Day 4's is earlier: it says only that the heartbeat is louder there. Keep, or swap for a plainer clue?
3. **Day 5's slates** are new objects in the tower. They make the B reading ("only a guess") worth arguing about without saying the count is wrong. If new objects in the sky-watchers' room feel like too much, I would swap them for a B clue about cloud through the open roof.
