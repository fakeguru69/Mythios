# Campfire callbacks: rewrite draft (15 Oct 2026)

**Status: DRAFT for Fake Guru. Nothing in the game has changed.** The live lines are in `src/data/campfire-days-v23.json` (field `callback`) and `docs/canon/mythios-campfire-table-v2.md`. If he approves, both get the new lines together.

**What a callback is:** the one line Garrick says at the fire under "Garrick remembers", tying today to an earlier day.

**The problem (audit `docs/audits/point-6-campfire-discussions.md` §A3):** almost every line is "Yesterday X. Today Y." That retells the story. A good callback puts two true things side by side so the friends notice a pattern or a question, and lets them work out the meaning.

**How the new lines were written**
- Two true facts, side by side. No conclusion.
- Only things the player has already seen by that evening. Chapter facts every player sees come first. Notice lines (one Attribute only), [PRESS ON] extras and Fan Out leads were avoided, because not every friend sees them.
- Garrick's voice: warm, plain, one or two short sentences. He never promises or predicts. No "maybe" or "perhaps". No sarcasm. He never points at himself, at the Order's hidden aims, at whose blood is whose, or at the finale.
- First-use gates kept (CLAUDE.md §6). "Malakor" never appears. Fragment texts are not quoted, so no lock word is given away. Days are named by number.

**Count:** 3 connect, 32 only recap, 1 has none (Week 1 Day 1).
