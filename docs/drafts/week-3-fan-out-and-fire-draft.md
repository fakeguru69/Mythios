How a lead works: the button shows the **place**. The player picks one of its two **rolls**. The **clue always comes back**. A pass also gives the first-letter **hint** for that fragment word. A fail gives the clue with a **late line** and no hint. **Leans** is for checking only and is never shown.

Each day has one lead leaning A, one leaning B, and one that complicates both. Two leads hint word 1, one hints word 2.

**Wren's chips are counted in canon** (three on Day 4, the rest on Day 5, eight given back at the Gathering). So no lead finds a new chip. The Day 4 leads look only at the three already in hand.

## Week 3 question (NEW; the case so far)

**What do they need Wren for?**

*Fits the episode's investigation line: "A third name, a girl called Wren, carried off to 'somewhere warm'." Who she is and where "somewhere warm" is are both answered early (Day 1 and Day 6). What stays open all week is the mother's line on Day 2, "they'd only need her the once", and the Day 2 Pull, "What does anyone need a child for, once?" The Gathering answers it: "To wake him."*

## Doubts for Fake Guru

1. **Day 6 is seen after dark.** On a failed climb canon says "nothing to see from it but black on black". Two Day 6 leads (No horses, Smoke from the crack) need eyes on the Kiln. The smoke comes after the lamp, which every player sees, but the tethered horses need light. My suggestion: keep them, and add one line to the late set for this day ("By starlight, you can just make it out."). Or swap "No horses" for another lead on a failed climb.

3. **Day 2's made bed** shows the cart that canon only tells of through the boy ("left standing empty"). The party never goes to it in the chapter. Fine to let a lead walk there, or keep every lead inside the village?
