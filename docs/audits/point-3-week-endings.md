## Part B: checked against the other AIs (`Audit_3.md`)

### B1. Where they corrected me

- **I ranked Week 5 second. Grok, DeepSeek and Meta rank it last, and they are right.** The watchman's look is answered "no, you weren't found" the next morning (W6D1), so the threat is defused at once. And the reveal comes from a fragment the party already holds, so nothing new is unlocked. I now rank it 5th.

### B2. Their best proposals

- **Week 5 rewrite (Grok), for your decision since it changes story text:** "The fifth lies in your cut hands and stays cold. Garrick binds the linen and does not tell you it will be all right. Across the square a watchman draws the chalk through another stroke. He does not look up. He does not have to. Seven left. The hall doors are warm from the inside. Garrick says, 'We are not hidden. We are early.'" W6D1 would then open on the cold Stone and the arrow, not on "Are you found?".
- **Week 4:** the dawn that stops halfway is never mentioned again (Grok). Same as my finding.
- **Week 1:** the horse vanishes, and the recap files the rescue as done (Grok). Same as my finding.

### B4. Where I differ

- **Meta puts Week 4 first** ("the only true paradigm shift"). The image is strong, but the app's next morning drops it, so in the build it loses its pull.
- **ChatGPT says Week 6 "lacks consequences".** Agree it lacks an aftermath. Canon has one designed (the Garrick Recap); it just is not built.
