## 2. Per night

**5-1**
- The fork is one-sided. At Gathering 4 the party read "The fifth heeds no element at all, but blood alone" (E4 line 930), so B is already known. The payoff is real: at Gathering 5 it is "not the shape on the rubbing… does not feel like earth or wind or water or fire" (E5 line 809), and the line returns at Gathering 6 (E6 line 816).
- "The crisp edge" (leans B) is about the king's erased record, not about the Stone. "The rime stays" (leans A) mixes up the cold of the fragment's housing with the Stone itself. Neither clue bears on the fork.
- "Every window lit" is the best clue. It calls back Reedham's dark-moon lamp custom (E4 line 299) and quietly plants the dark moon before Gathering 5.
- The meaning question ("Take what, and from whom?") pays off at Gathering 6 with "The power that gives is also the power that takes" (E6 line 967).
- The callback, Meira's missing fifth beside the cut-out line, is good. The nudge is generic.

**5-2**
- The fork asks about the dogs, which is local colour. No episode text resolves it. The nearest echo is the dead walking to the hall (E6 Day 6), which is never tied back to the dogs.
- "One bite" (leans A) does not show the dogs being called. "Scattered" says the tracks came from the north, which argues against "calls them north" as much as for B.
- The nudge asks the fork word for word.
- The meaning question pays off strongly in Gathering 5's Verse of the Binding: "his own, who had held his wall" (E5 line 756).

**5-3**
- Both readings hold up. Day 5 implies the answer, the Order camped in the city for generations (E5 line 489), but no line ever says who bars the gate or why.
- "One trodden path" (leans A) is real evidence: a path from the bar to the smoke points to Day 5's fire. "Knocked out", the stair broken on purpose long ago, is new information and truly ambiguous.
- "Still white" (leans B): a new bar does not show that it keeps something in.
- The nudge repeats the day's Pull ("Who bars a dead city…", E5 line 320).
- The meaning question is excellent. "None of us gave to the fifth" is one of the four lines laid out at the Gathering 6 reveal (E6 line 820).

**5-4**
- The fork is a perception check rather than an interpretation. It does pay off: at Gathering 5 the soldiers walk (E5 line 698), the floor notice says "Its helm was turned to the hall" (E5 line 790), and they are moving by Week 6 Day 2.
- "Unbroken dust" pays off exactly in Gathering 5's Agility [LOOK CLOSER]: "Their footprints… begin in the middle of the street. Before tonight they had not taken a step" (E5 line 795). "No two alike" is honestly ambiguous.
- The callback (the dogs stopped, the soldiers stopped) is one of the best links between days in the two weeks. The weathervane riddle fits.
- The meaning question ("Why not?") is never answered beyond sentiment.

**5-5**
- This is a real mystery question, and it pays off. Week 6 Day 5 shows the night was set by the moon's cycle since his fall, and the 6-5 callback says so outright: "counted down for generations… lands on the same night". The Watch reinforces it: "let no night pass uncounted" (E5 line 876).
- The clues undercut it. "One hand" is labelled A, yet "gone over many times, never redrawn" argues the board is old, which supports B. "Every head lifts" (leans B) says nothing about when the count began.
- "More bedrolls" is good foreshadowing: twice as many at the fire on Week 6 Day 3 (E6 line 250).
- The nudge ("what ends when the count ends?") is the best nudge in the two weeks.

**5-6**
- The fork is dead on arrival. The chapter says "They kept the fifth inside their own wall, and watched him from it" (E5 line 618), so the honest answer is "both", and the fragment says so too (E5 line 540).
- The meaning question quotes the last fragment and asks "Why there, and why that night?". That runs ahead of Gathering 5, which is built so the party bleeds on the Stone first and finds this fragment only afterwards (E5 lines 855–866, notes line 931). Asked the night before, it takes the edge off the failed attempt.
- "Two shallow dips" pays off in Week 6 Day 1's Craft notice (two hollows, E6 line 57).
- The callback, every map read aloud before tonight, is a pointed and fair hint at how the Order has kept up.

**6-1**
- The fork is resolved before the fire. At dusk the chapter states "the Order has never known this room was there. It still does not." (E6 line 113), and the summary card says "you are not found". The fire comes after the chapter.
- The leans are wrong. "One look down the hill", the watchman turning to the wall before he strikes, is labelled A but shows suspicion (B). "No light inside" is labelled B but shows there is nothing for them to see.
- "The old soot", a candle hidden from the slit, explains how the Seekers stayed hidden for a lifetime. It is good world-building that has nothing to do with the fork.
- The meaning question ("Why spare anyone?") is one of the season's best set-ups: the servants are Garrick's house (E6 line 875, Match line 879). Together with his "Somewhere they'd never be found" the same day, it is a fair Week 6 clue.
- The nudge only asks where the arrow points, which the Pull answers.

**6-2**
- Both readings hold up. Gathering 6 answers B, the hollowed "still standing their watch" between the party and the throne (E6 line 725), and A is partly true too (they move on the heartbeat).
- "Swept racks" and "Every foot at once" are real evidence for their leans. "Bowls never filled" is moving but bears on nothing.
- The nudge repeats the fork.
- The meaning question ("How could a mark help anyone find one?") goes to the heart of the season: "You were the key" (E6 line 938).

**6-3**
- A and B can both be true at once. B ("There is, and it isn't ours") gives away the next day's beat, "There is another door. And the Order has it." (E6 line 413).
- These are the strongest clues in the two weeks. "Warm low down" fits the king lying on the floor (E6 line 769). "Clean robes" fits the robed faithful at Gathering 6. "Unbroken drift" agrees with the chapter.
- The callback repeats the chapter. The blanket riddle is weak.
- The meaning question ("What kept them from lifting him?") is never answered.

**6-4**
- The fork is about timing, which Days 5 and 6 settle (A). Meanwhile the day's real question, "The Seekers sealed the doors they knew about" (E6 line 409) and who has the other door, gets no fork. That is the strongest moment for an attentive player in Week 6, and the fire looks the other way.
- "Untouched grit" and "Two hollows" barely bear on their leans. "Louder here" is good: the king is behind the rock.
- The nudge ("Why keep a soldier at it?") is a sharp question, and Gathering 6 answers it implicitly: the dead guard the king.
- The meaning question, why leaving a way in needs forgiving, is answered by the finale itself, because the Seekers' door is how the fifth reaches him. It is never said aloud.

**6-5**
- The fork is never answered. No verse says whether the Seekers chose the dark moon.
- "Sums begun again" is labelled B, but long calculations argue for choosing the night (A).
- The callback states 5-5's answer outright, which is a good payoff for 5-5.
- The meaning question ("Be there to do what?") is well judged: the Seekers' "be there" and the Order's Watch ("the faithful gather where he fell", E5 line 878) mean opposite things.

**6-6**
- This is the right last-night question: does the party sense the night is not theirs? B is the truth behind "You were the key". A is literally what happens.
- The nudge ("What do they know that we don't?") points squarely at the trap, which is fair for the night before the finale.
- There is a mild tension between "The swept fan", which implies the door has swung before, and 6-4's "Your boots are the first marks". It reads as old grit and can be squared, but a sharp player will query it.
- The callback is filler. The "story" riddle suits the last night.

## 3. Cross-night findings

**The five biggest problems, ranked**

1. **Nothing a player pins ever pays off.** The fork pins, the meaning pins and the case-so-far lines are never read back after the reveal. The Gathering 6 reveal (E6 lines 814–842) and Garrick's Recap (`after-the-finale.md`) list only his lines. The friend who pinned 6-6 B, or wrote "the cup isn't for Meira", gets nothing. CLAUDE.md §11 still lists "the read-backs of the week's pins" as open. This is the main reason the fire risks feeling like busywork.
2. **Four forks are already answered before the fire.** 5-1 was answered by the torn page at Gathering 4, 5-6 by the Day 6 chapter, 6-1 by the dusk [PLANT], and 6-3's reading A by the chapter. The fire runs after the chapter, so every fork has to be checked against the whole day, Pull and summary card included.
3. **The forks do not build one investigation.** The Week 5 forks cover dogs, a bar, a helm and a count; only 5-1 and 5-5 serve the week question, "What will it take for the fifth to end this?". Not one Week 6 fork asks "What will the fifth do in his hall?", or whose blood it wants. The season's most telling Week 6 evidence lands on nights whose fork looks elsewhere: "Somewhere they'd never be found" on 6-1, "sealed the doors they knew about" on 6-4, and the cup on 6-6.
4. **The hidden leans cannot be trusted.** 6-1-2 is swapped. 5-5-1 contradicts itself. 6-5-2 supports the reading it is not labelled with. 5-1-1, 5-1-2 and 5-2-2 bear on other questions. No Week 5–6 lead has a `bearsOnFork` note to check them against.
5. **Repetition and misplaced content.** The nudges repeat the fork (5-2, 6-2) or the Pull (5-3, 6-1). The callbacks repeat the chapter (6-1, 6-3, 6-6). The 5-6 meaning question runs ahead of Gathering 5's turn. Four nights never get an answer: 5-2, 6-5, and the 5-4 and 6-3 meaning questions. The as-played clues also differ from `mythios-campfire-table-v2.md` for 5-3, 5-4, 6-3 and 6-5; the table still names the old [PRESS ON] details.

**The three best nights**
- **6-3:** every clue is evidence, and two pay off physically at Gathering 6 (the warm floor, the robes).
- **5-4:** a strong callback, "Unbroken dust" paid off exactly at Gathering 5, and a riddle that fits.
- **5-5:** a real question about the Order's purpose, with a fine nudge and an earned answer two weeks of play later (the 6-5 callback).

The meaning questions are the strongest layer overall. Those for 5-1, 5-2, 5-3, 6-1, 6-2 and 6-6 all lead into the finale's reveals.

## 4. Direction for fixing

**Cheap fixes (data only, no new mechanics)**
- Retarget the forks for 5-1, 5-6 and 6-1 at what the day leaves open. For 5-6, for example, ask what the party will do with the fifth, not why the room exists. For 6-1, ask what the Seekers feared about whoever escaped. Recheck every fork against the full chapter, Pull and summary card.
- Reword 6-3 B so it does not give away Day 4.
- Fix the leans for 6-1-2, 5-5-1 and 6-5-2. Fill `bearsOnFork` for every Week 5–6 lead, so each clue can be checked against the fork it is meant to bear on.
- Rewrite the nudges that repeat the fork or the Pull, so each points at one piece of evidence.
- Replace the filler callbacks (6-1, 6-3, 6-6) with links back to earlier weeks.
- Move or soften the 5-6 meaning question so it does not run ahead of Gathering 5. One option is to ask about "joined" rather than "why there and that night".
- Bring `mythios-campfire-table-v2.md` in line with the played data.

**Deeper fixes (need Fake Guru's decision)**
- **A read-back after the reveal.** After "You never lied to us", or on the Gathering 6 summary card next to Garrick's Recap, show each friend what they pinned on the nights that mattered (6-6, 5-5, 6-2) and the meaning lines they wrote, beside what turned out to be true. This rewards attentive players without grading anyone.
- **A Week 6 fork ladder** that climbs towards "What will the fifth do in his hall?" and "whose blood?": the Order's other door on 6-4, why they spare the servants on 6-1, and 6-6 as it stands. Each should rest on true evidence and never name Garrick.
- **Answers for the questions the story leaves open.** Either give 5-2 and 6-5 a line of payoff in Week 6 or at Gathering 6, or swap those forks for ones the story does answer.
- **Story changes need Fake Guru.** Any change to story text, Garrick's lines or fragments is his decision under CLAUDE.md §8. The fixes above touch only Campfire data.
