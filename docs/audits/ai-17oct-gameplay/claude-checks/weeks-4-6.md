# Claude's independent gameplay check: weekdays of Weeks 4 to 6 (17 Oct)

This check read only the game's data and code. It did not read Claude's own write-up or the outside reports.

## Facts
- **Notice:** each player sees only the line for their highest Attribute; the second sentence needs a score of 51 or more (`ChapterScriptParts.tsx:162-177`). No screen ever shows a friend's Notice line. The only link is Garrick's fire nudge, which names a friend by Attribute.
- **A failed Challenge:** costs 150g and swaps a paragraph.
  - The "Someone at the fire may have seen it" promise is kept in the demo (`StoryAndPartyTabs.tsx:1898-1913`) but **not online**: `OnlineCampfire.tsx:285` lists only who passed.
- **A lost fight:** drops the "won" line and nothing more. Weekday fights are won 95 to 99% of the time.
- **What carries from one day to a later one, in these 18 days:**
  - only the two Gathering carries: Wren carried on W4D1, and +2 on W6D1's card;
  - **nothing a player does on a weekday reaches a later weekday.**
- **DC:** 8 in Week 4, then 9 in every day from W5D1 to W6D6. Tier bonuses keep rising, so **the play gets easier as the story darkens**. W6D6's card says "The hardest yet." on the same DC 9.

## Totals
- 41 dead taps.
- 1 flavour choice and 4 real choices; only W5D2's changes play.
- Results that set who strikes first: W4D5, W5D2, W6D6.
- Attribute slots across the 16 cards: Aim 7, Craft 6, Agility 6, Intellect 5, Might 5, Charm 3. A Charm-first player can use their best skill on only 3 of 16 cards.
- The day's shape is nearly always: read, Notice, a two-option Challenge, maybe [PRESS ON], the fragment tap, the Pull. From W6D1, STRIKE THE PAGE is added every day.

## Judgement
1. **Only the story escalates.** No new weekday mechanic arrives in Weeks 4 to 6, and the odds improve.
2. **The countdown is a ritual, not tension.** Week 6's failures describe new danger that never touches a later roll. The existing `harderIf` can read any claim key, so a W6D2 fail could make W6D3 one step harder; the +1 is Fake Guru's number.
3. **The Notice lines are good and mostly clue-bearing**, often on tonight's question, but friends can never compare them. **The fix:** put "what I noticed" on the fire board.
4. **The fragments and leads are mostly well tied to their scenes.** The weaker fits are 4-1, 4-2 and 5-3.
5. **The most valuable change:** weekday results set claims that later weekdays read, together with building the fire share online.
