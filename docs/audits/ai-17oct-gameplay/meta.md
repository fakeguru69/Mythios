# Tales of the Dark Moon — Gameplay Quality Report

**Sources used:** `gameplay-quality-17oct.md` Sections 2-5, `gameplay-audit-prompt.md` criteria list. No playthrough text files were provided, so claims are limited to rhythm numbers and mechanic summary — **(guess)** where extrapolation needed is marked.

### 1. The verdict in five lines

The gameplay is not good enough yet to carry six weeks, but the fix is small and clear.
It is more story than game — right for friends who want a mystery to share, not a grind.
The rhythm is story-heavy (577 words, 6.5 taps per weekday) but most taps are "go on", not decisions.
Every button must become a decision, a reveal, or a dramatic beat: fewer, heavier taps.
The single most important change: make fails and Notice details matter tomorrow and at the Gathering, so uneven information drives the Campfire.

### 3. The core loop

**What you gave me says:**

> Weekday: ~577 words / 6.5 taps / ~3 min read / 8-12 min total → chapter with paid Challenge/fight, optional PRESS ON, Pull, then Seeker Fragment scrambled words → last light (3 leads roll or skip) → Campfire (5-6 things to tap/write) [gameplay-quality-17oct.md §3]

**What works:**
- **Day:** Story-first shape is right for this audience. 577 words is not point-to-point.
- **Week:** Fragments building to map is natural habit loop — "What makes our gameplay work" calls it best piece.
- **Season:** Gathering as live together payoff with Approach vote, Battle lockstep, United Effort two bars, Match, Ask, table question is most real play.

**What is missing:**
- **Day:** No decision inside story. Decision = which Attribute to roll, and players will nearly always pick best [§3 Problem is what taps are]. No room for story between buttons because buttons aren't story.
- **Week:** Evening is where thinking happens, but weekday shaped as story THEN game THEN talk — not woven [§3]. Nothing in day's scene asks for fragment.
- **Season:** Weekdays change meaning of Gathering, not how it plays. Gold/gear loop is closed: dice move gold, gold buys gear fights barely need [§3 Rolls that rarely change].

### 5. Pacing: story against play

Using your numbers [§3]:

- **Weekday:** 577w story with 6.5 taps = ~88w per tap average. Longest stretch 409w (W3D2) — too long if no decision. Shortest 85w (W6D4) — too short, feels point-to-point. Goal: 150-220w between *meaningful* taps.
- **Fix:** Remove 3-4 go-on taps per day. Keep pause taps only before reveal (e.g., before fragment find) and after death. Your earlier reviewer fear "moving point to point" is wrong on word count but right on tap meaning: it's not too many points, it's empty points.

- **Gathering:** 880-2230w reading. G2 and G6 ~10 min before any talk/play. Fails criterion 7 (quiet beat at big turn) and 9 (build).
- **Fix:** Break G2/G6: 300w → Approach vote → 300w → Battle → rest. Never 1000w uninterrupted.

- **Game woven through story?** No now: story, then game, then talk [§3]. Should be woven: fragment word *in* chapter, Challenge attribute *chooses* which scene line you get, Notice shared *at* Campfire immediately, not next day.

### 6. The eight questions, answered

1. **More game or story, balance right?** More story than game, and that is right for friends wanting shared mystery [§1 What kind of game]. Balance fails because buttons don't carry story weight. Keep story amount, make each tap story-heavy.

2. **Enough room for story between buttons?** Yes, too much empty room. 577w / 6.5 taps shows long reads earn it [§3], but taps are go-on. Need fewer, heavier taps, not more story.

3. **Woven through or after?** Must be woven. Now Challenge, fragment, last light happen *after* chapter. Move at least one into scene: fragment word as name in scene, Challenge as how you get clue.

4. **Does every button earn place?** No. Most don't. 8 flavour choices, 3-4 go-ons per day, last light roll, PRESS ON safe version. Merge go-ons, cut flavour or make them change Notice/Pull, make PRESS ON risk shared detail.

5. **Do dice matter?** Barely. Fights won 95-99%, Challenge fail only 11/29 loses info, nothing carries next day, gold buys gear fights barely need [§3, §2 Criterion 2]. Need: fail = different scene + lost clue for Match, and carry -1 to Gathering.

6. **Do friends need each other?** Not enough. Weekdays solo, Notice private, Relic gift not plan, Campfire forms too many. They hold different detail but nothing asks to pool [§4 Notice]. Fix: uneven info as core — share Notice to get +2 map, Relic needed to call help, theory right = bonus.

7. **Does week build to Gathering?** Weakly. Only map +2 bonus mechanically changes play. Need weekdays change *how* Gathering plays: fails = worse start bar, Notice pooled = Ask keys, Relic saved = help-call.

8. **Would I come back every day for six weeks?** Might drift at Week 3-4. Reason to return: Pull hook + half-done fragment puzzle + friend theory [§4]. Drift risk: routine (same fight ending, same fragment find, same evening) [§2 Criterion 8], low stakes. Need variety rotation + stakes that carry.

### 7. Proposals, ranked — ten changes that most improve play

**1. Make every fail leave a mark that carries.**
- Change: Challenge/fight fail shows different scene line, loses one Match-relevant detail, gives -1 to next day's first roll or United Effort start.
- Why: Criteria 2, 9, 1. Dice matter, week builds.
- Cost: Medium (new fail text across season, one number)
- Risk: More text; may feel punitive. Mitigate: fail still progresses, just costs position/info.

**2. Uneven information as core loop — Notice sharing required.**
- Change: Campfire auto-shows "Only you saw: X" with Share button. Map +2 only if 3+ different Notices shared.
- Why: 5, 3, 1. Friends need each other, mystery is game, decisions matter.
- Cost: Medium (new Campfire UI piece + text reuse)
- Risk: Feels forced. Mitigate: small bonus, not block.

**3. Fewer, heavier taps — cut go-ons.**
- Change: Merge 3-4 go-on per weekday. Keep pause before fragment/reveal.
- Why: 7, 1. Rhythm, earn place.
- Cost: Small
- Risk: Long reads feel long. Test 150-220w target.

**4. Weave fragment into chapter.**
- Change: One scrambled word appears highlighted in chapter as place/name. Player taps to unscramble? Second word stays in Bag.
- Why: 10, 4, 3. Rules tell story, puzzle feels good.
- Cost: Medium (mark text)
- Risk: Miss if skim. Mitigate: highlight + last light hint.

**5. Fight endings variety + consequence.**
- Change: 3 endings rotating: escape, leaves clue, captured. 6/7 same "silent man runs off" fixed.
- Why: 8, 2. Variety, stakes.
- Cost: Small (2 extra lines per fight)
- Risk: Extra writing but low.

**6. PRESS ON risks shared detail.**
- Change: Success = private extra detail needed for map, fail = you drop your Notice for all to see (costs you uniqueness but helps group).
- Why: 1, 2, 5. Real decision, friends need you.
- Cost: Small
- Risk: Players avoid. Make success valuable.

**7. Campfire cut to 3 tabs + theory payoff.**
- Change: Theories, Fragments, Notes. Right theory = Garrick nod + Match +2 at Gathering.
- Why: 6, 3, 13. Async works, mystery, habit.
- Cost: Medium
- Risk: Less space but clearer.

**8. Challenge attribute = how you learn.**
- Change: Not pass/fail, but which detail you get. Strength moves stone → sees sigil, Sage reads → hears whisper. Both needed for Match.
- Why: 1, 10, 12. Decision, fiction, growth.
- Cost: Medium
- Risk: Doubles Notice lines. Use existing lines where possible.

**9. Relic help-call economy.**
- Change: Calling for help in Battle costs 1 Relic (any friend can pay). Giving Relic becomes planning before Gathering.
- Why: 5, 2. Friends need, stakes.
- Cost: Small (number change)
- Risk: May feel pay-to-win. Make +4 but not required.

**10. Break Gathering long reads.**
- Change: Never >400w without vote/play. G2/G6 split: story → Approach → story → Battle.
- Why: 7, 9.
- Cost: Small (reorder)
- Risk: Pacing change needs timing test.

### 8. What to test with real players

1. **Do friends actually talk at fire?** Watch Week 1 fire: how many pins, replies, shares? If <2 per person, forms too many. Need prompt "What did only you see?"
2. **Does day feel long or short?** Time 4 friends doing W1D1-D3 alone. Ask "where did you want a button?" If >3 min without decision, merge.
3. **Is tapping through fight boring or tense?** Watch faces during Battle. If tapping without reading, add story line per round.
4. **Does Gathering fit in 30 min?** Time G1 and G2 live. If reading >10 min before talk, split as proposed.
5. **Does fail feel unfair or exciting?** A/B test fail that costs info vs gold. Ask after: "Did you want to come back to fix it?"

### 9. What must not change

- **Seeker Fragments + maps:** Best piece, real puzzle, twist model where solving shows Order the way.
- **Campfire theory pinning + seeing friends' pins:** Mystery at its best, book-club core.
- **United Effort two bars + turns + changing story lines:** Truly shared, felt win/loss that sometimes carries.
- **Match rewarding attention:** Attention pays, core to mystery.
- **Daily 8-12 min async + weekly 20-30 live shape:** Right for fixed party 2-6, phone habit.
