### Patterns

1. The season's big mystery is very well built. Each week changes what you thought you knew.
2. Weeks 1–4 repeat one shape: new child, family visit, trail, map, rescue. Canon already lists "the rescue loop visible by Week 3" for after launch.
3. "The family of a missing child" repeats: W1D4, W1D5, W2D2, W3D2, W4D3.
4. In Week 6 the questions turn practical; the countdown carries the tension. Canon lists "Week 6 Days 1 to 3 feeling alike".
5. The cheapest big win: build [PRESS ON] and the "look here" choices. That restores each † day's twist in its own story, with no new writing.

**Suggestions for weak days (ideas only, for Fake Guru to decide):** W3D4, make the bone die the turn ("whoever slept here had long nights too"); W4D3, lead with "they are waiting for something"; W6D1–D3, give each a different feeling (fear, horror, dread).
