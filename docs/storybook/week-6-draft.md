**How to read this.** Quote blocks are what MYTHIOS says. `[BUTTONS]` are what the player taps. Branch labels are in plain italics. Lines in italics outside the quote blocks are stage notes for the build, not player text. Cards, notice lines, fight cards and summary cards are kept as canon has them, except where a "What changed" note says otherwise. Mei, Dev and Jo stand in for the real friends. They act, and never speak.

**This pass, in short.** Week 6 is quiet, exact and frightening, and its Gathering is the best writing in the season, so this is the lightest pass yet. The audits' real catches are fixed: "his dead brother" on Day 1, which could be read as a new fact; Day 1's vague "the start of their way" and its six-word pass line; Day 2's bare opening, its second "Nobody breathes." in two days, and "the others", which reads as the player's own friends; Day 3's opening, which gave the time back to front; the two Aim notices with no second sentence; the flat "where no light reaches without a lamp" on Day 4; the finale's one-line United Effort endings; the unclear "It" when a Stone is struck from the ring; [DO NOTHING] with no line at all; and the bracketed stage direction shown above the untorn page. I also caught slips of my own finding. On Day 1 nobody puts out the lamp the watchman saw. On Day 5 the moon is "rising" after a failure has already burned half the night. At the Gathering the party sees the moon "above you" from deep in a crack, and "overhead" from inside a roofed hall. If the party holds the fifth back, it later lies in Garrick's hand with no word of how it left yours. "None of them has ever seen his face" could mean the king. The two Match quote-backs say "on the second day of this week", which is game talk. And Garrick's Day 4 line says one of that day's two fragment lock words aloud. Each day now has a time of day and a close. The reveal keeps every word: every Garrick line, every scripture line, the four messages, the untorn page, the last sliver and the names. The narration around it gets room, and loses its dashes and contractions, as in Weeks 2 to 5. Garrick has no new lines. Before the reveal he gets small actions on Days 2, 3 and 5 and one line of narration round his last warm words; after it, one action that is only courtesy.

## Week 6 daily recaps

*One per day, for a friend who missed the day or wants the story so far. Plain past-tense sentences, one idea to a sentence, about 100 to 125 words, at most five sentences, each ending on the day's Pull; the Gathering about 210. Each tells only what every branch shares. Friends are never named. No recap gives away a lock word of the day it covers. "Malakor" is not used: canon's Week 6 text never names him to the players (see item 15). The tally: six left after Day 1, then five, four, three, two, one, and none on the threshold.*

**Day 1. Six nights**
> The watchman who looked up at your window raised the alarm, and all night the Order's lanterns hunted along the wall. Twice their boots went right past the hidden room, and twice they walked on, because from the square every slit in that wall looks the same. In the stone niche where the fifth had lain, you found a fragment, and under the slit window an arrow scratched long ago pointed down into the wall. At dusk the Order struck its board, and you struck the dead watchman's page to six while Garrick watched. The hall is shut fast, but the Seekers left a way in somewhere. The arrow points down, to the defenders' barracks under the wall.

**Day 2. Five nights**
> In the morning the crossroads where the king's soldiers had stood for centuries was empty. In the barracks under the wall you cleared a fallen beam, and one of the armoured dead came down the stair and stood in the doorway, looking past you at the stone. When the heartbeat came, it turned and climbed away up the hill. You found a fragment in the hearth of the small room where the Seekers slept, passed the rest of the soldiers three streets nearer the hall, and struck the page to five at dusk. The dead are not coming for you. They are going to him. Tonight, after dark, across the great square to his walls.

**Day 3. Four nights**
> At dusk you struck the page to four, and after dark you went up to the great square, where twice as many watchmen now sat round the Order's fire. Their board showed four strokes left, the same as your page. You crossed sixty paces of open ground to the hall's great wall and laid a palm on it. The stone was warm, as if something behind it had been warm for a very long time, and the heartbeat came through it into your hand. There is no way in at the front. The hall's back wall runs into the hill, so tomorrow, the cracked hill behind it.

**Day 4. Three nights**
> By noon the sky was as dim as dusk, and on the cracked hill behind the hall you found one crack that ran straight, as if it had been ruled. One of the king's soldiers stood in its mouth with its back to the hall, and you broke it. Deep inside you found a fragment, and the crack ended in blank rock beside a carving of a full moon with its whole face scored out. Garrick reminded you that the Seekers shut only the doors they knew about, so there is another door into the hall, and the Order has it. That night you struck the page to three. The same scored moon is cut high on the moon-tower at the city's edge.

**Day 5. Two nights**
> The heartbeat no longer waited for dusk, and by noon the ground trembled with it. At the top of the moon-tower, where the old sky-watchers cut every moon into the wall, you found the one cut deeper than the rest, the full moon struck out on the night he fell, with bare stone after it. Counting forward with a fingertip at their spacing, you came all the way round the room to the doorpost, and that same moon comes again in two nights. As the nearly full moon came up over the open roof, Garrick said quietly, “It always comes back,” and you struck the page to two. You have the hour, one piece of the map is left, and tomorrow is the last day.

**Day 6. One night**
> The street to the hill was full of the king's soldiers, walking slowly up to his hall, and you crossed between their ranks. At the mouth of the crack one of them had turned back, and you broke it where the last one fell. At the dead end the last fragment made the map whole, and it showed that the blank rock is a door, marked with one word, *here*, beside the scored moon. Back in the wall room you struck the page to one, and Garrick poured the last of his flask, with one cup more on the sill of the slit window. Tomorrow night, when the moon goes dark, you go through the Seekers' door into his hall with the fifth in your hands.

**Gathering 6. His hall**
> On the last night the Seekers' door in the hill opened for your blood, and the last stroke went through the watch-page as the moon began to go dark. You fought through the faithful and the hollowed in his hall and found the king asleep on the floor where he fell, with the four Stones glowing in a ring around him. Your blood did nothing to the fifth. Garrick said, “Let me,” and the fifth answered his blood, and its light pinned you where you knelt. Moon-blood was never a word for you. It was the king's own title, and then his house's, and Garrick is the last of that house and the Order is his. He had never lied to you, and his people killed Meira on the road. From his own whole copy of their book he read the untorn page, which ends “and the throne rise whole.” The fifth was never going to stop the waking. It finished it. He set the extra cup beside the king, because it had always been for him, and closed the king's hand around the fifth. “You were the key,” he said. Then the moon went dark, and the king opened his eyes and looked at you, knowing Seeker blood. “You saved every child you were sent to save,” Garrick said.
