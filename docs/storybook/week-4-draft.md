*Not yet approved. Waiting on Fake Guru's choices in "Things I was unsure of" at the end.*

*A rewrite of the player-facing text of `docs/canon/episode-4-the-water-stone.md`, Days 1 to 7, in the storybook voice of `docs/style/storybook-voice.md`, matched to the approved `docs/storybook/week-1-draft.md`, `week-2-draft.md` and `week-3-draft.md`. 16 Oct 2026. Nothing else in the repo has been changed.*

**How to read this.** Quote blocks are what MYTHIOS says. `[BUTTONS]` are what the player taps. Branch labels are in plain italics. Lines in italics outside the quote blocks are stage notes for the build, not player text. Cards, notice lines, fight cards and summary cards are kept as canon has them, except where a "What changed" note says otherwise. Mei, Dev and Jo stand in for the real friends. They act, and never speak.

**This pass, in short.** Week 4 was already plain and well built, so this is refinement, not reinvention. The audits' real catches are fixed: the vague walk back to Ashcombe, the fork on Day 2 where the player could not tell who found the riders' tracks, the jump from the doorpost to the notches on Day 3, the thin pass lines on Days 2, 3 and 4, the two fragment finds with no cold on Days 4 and 5, the two-line opening of Day 6, the Approach failure line that repeated its card, the Battle opening that copied Gathering 1's pattern, the notice line that said the Kiln's faithful all fell, and the stage brackets shown inside the torn page. I also caught three slips of my own finding: Wren's mother kneeling "in the ash" on a road that is already past the grass, "You lay the six out" written one line before the `[LAY THEM OUT]` button, and Garrick bailing "halfway out" after the skiff has already arrived. Each day now has a time of day and a close. The big moments get room: the empty place where he knelt, Wren's homecoming, the eight notches, the boy's small prints, the flame in the bell chamber, the falling dust, the pen. Garrick has no new lines. He gets four small kind actions, and his coat comes home from Wren's shoulders.

**Day 5. The reed island**

**Day 6. The tower at night**
