# Garrick's spoken lines in the story text: likeability audit (16 Oct, revised)

Sources read: `docs/canon/episode-1-the-list.md` to `episode-6-the-finale.md` (Days 1 to 6 and the Gathering), and `docs/canon/garrick-deception-table.md` in full. I also checked `src/data/garrickRouter.ts` and the Campfire table so that nothing proposed here repeats a line already used.

**Scope:** Garrick's spoken lines only. The narration and the other characters are being audited separately. Where a load-bearing line can only be helped by its narration, I list that at the end as a handover, not as a proposal.

**Length aim** (designer, 16 Oct): not a rushed one-liner and not long. Texture, never padding. Short stays only where short is the joke or the weight ("Three more.", "Needed doing.", "Let me.", "I won't promise you that.", "It's terrible. Drink it anyway.").

**Overall:** his spoken lines are in good shape. The best of them are already warm, dry and a pleasure to read: "That's a rock. Split lightning-wise…", "Worst guests I've ever had. Good tippers, though.", "I kept a tally behind my bar for years…", "My knees have opinions about this glass." Most of his thinner lines are load-bearing, so their words must stay; that leaves few lines to rewrite.

**Count:** I checked 64 spoken lines (Ep1 9, Ep2 13, Ep3 8, Ep4 10, Ep5 6, Ep6 18).
- **3** lines: I would change the wording.
- **6** lines: load-bearing and thin, so they keep their words, and I hand them to the narration audit below.
- **55** kept as written. This includes every line after "You never lied to us" in Week 6, which is cold on purpose.

## Where Garrick could gain a small warm or wry moment (suggestions only)

None of these days has a Garrick moment now. Each suggestion is true, makes no promise or prediction, repeats no bank line that is already used, and respects the first-use gates. "You've not eaten. Sit." is already used by the Campfire and the chat, so I left it out. Each is aimed at middle length: a line or two of his, with the moment that prompts it.

- **Ep2 D3 (Upriver):** to whoever is limping, as he falls into step beside them: *"Lean on me. The road's no shorter for being proud on it, and I'd rather walk slow than walk alone."* True as a wish; no fact about his past.
- **Ep3 D4 (the black ridges):** pouring the last of the tea, holding up the only pot there is: *"Same as last night?"* A bank line not yet used in any episode. It gives the "he remembers what you drink" note its first outing, and the single pot is the joke.
- **Ep5 D2 (the old kingdom's road, morning):** over a cold breakfast, with the birds silent: *"One of you snores like a drowned mule. I won't say who. Last night I slept with my head under my coat."* The first two sentences are the bank line, unused; the third is a true, small feeling. It lifts a grim morning without denying it.
- **Ep6 D2 (the barracks under the wall):** button `[YOU'RE FULL OF SURPRISES]`, when he turns out candle stubs from his coat in the dark: *"You've no idea."* Short is right here. It is the bank's best "true twice" line, and the last week before the reveal is where it hurts most on reread.

## Handover to the narration audit (load-bearing lines; his words stay)

These lines are thin, but they are load-bearing, so their wording must stay. Only the sentence around them can make them pleasant to read. I suggest these to whoever owns the narration, not as edits of mine.

- **Ep1 D2, "I've a business to run."**: he says it looking round an empty taproom, the [N] of them nursing one cup each. The joke is in the room.
- **Ep2 Gathering Ask, "I know the name."**: "He doesn't look at the page to check." Steady rather than cagey, and a fair-play clue.
- **Ep2 Gathering Ask, "Look at the ledger. Every name in it is a child's."**: he runs his thumb down the names without reading any aloud. Grave, not brusque.
- **Ep2 Gathering, "Here. Read every page."**: he hands it over the way he'd hand back a coat left on a hook.
- **Ep3 D1, the Ashcombe line**: he reads her shorthand "as easily as the day's bill of fare". This states plainly a clue the Table lists but the page never says.
- **Ep5 D4, the "say nothing" path**: "Neither does he." is followed at once by "For a long time he is quiet." The scene says the same thing twice.

## Lines that break a rule as written

**None.** No Garrick line in the six episode files lies, predicts, promises, denies knowing, uses sarcasm, invents a fact about him, or breaks a first-use gate ("Malakor" from Gathering 2; "Highwall" at W5D1). The post-reveal lines are courteous and cold, as required.

Four things to watch:

1. **"I doubt she thought I was listening." (Ep1 D2)** and **"…whoever can't be with us." (Ep1 D6, Ep5 D4).** Both are approved and true. But "doubt" and "whoever" are on the Guardrail's watch-list for improvised lines; if the chat's banned-phrase filter is ever widened to those words, these scripted lines must be exempt.
2. **The repeated "Malakor" (Ep4 Gathering).** He repeats the name and the grandmother line word for word, on purpose (Deception Table, heir clues). CLAUDE.md §6 says the chat deflects when he is asked again. Both can stand, but the difference is worth noting.
3. **Garrick lines missing from the Deception Table**, and so missing from Garrick's Recap:
   - "Three more." (Ep1 Gathering)
   - "I know what that page says." (Ep2 Gathering)
   - "One stayed close. It always did." (Ep6 Gathering)
   - "Your Seekers made their children the only key… You were the key." (Ep6 Gathering)
4. **"It always comes back" (Ep6 D5) and "It always did" (Ep6 Gathering)** echo each other. The echo looks deliberate and works on reread; keep both.
