# Prose audit: Weeks 5 and 6, weekdays (Days 1 to 6)

**Checked:** `docs/canon/episode-5-the-kings-stone.md` lines 32–616 and `docs/canon/episode-6-the-finale.md` lines 33–638. That is all twelve weekdays: openings, What you notice (72 lines), Challenge and fight cards, pass and fail branches, [PRESS ON] details, housings, the Pulls, the Day summary cards and the few lines spoken by people other than Garrick (the family man on W5D2, the watchmen). About 190 quote blocks in all.

**Overall verdict:** this is the strongest prose in the season. Most of it is already a pleasure to read: concrete, quiet and well paced, with clear stakes (Joss carried up the shingle, the war graves, the street of soldiers, the warm wall, counting the moons by fingertip). I left nearly all of it alone. The weak spots are small and fall into five kinds:
1. a gendered pronoun on a friend who could be anyone (a rule problem, W5D6);
2. a few one-line pass branches that feel rushed next to their rich fail branches;
3. unclear "they", "his" and "the others" where two groups are in play;
4. two What you notice lines with no second sentence (Adept players get nothing extra);
5. one awkward time order (W6D3 opening) and one phrase repeated on back-to-back days ("Nobody breathes").

About Garrick: none of these weekdays falls after the reveal. On Week 6 Days 1 to 6 he is still warm ("Give me a minute", "Drinks are on me"), and the narration around him reads that way. I propose no change to him or around him. Every `[PLANT]` line, every scripture line, every Garrick line and every number (nine, eight, seven, six…one) is unchanged below.

## Week 5

### W5D1 · The grey afternoon

**1. Fail branch (ep5 line 85)**
- CURRENT: "Nothing comes up that you can read. The stone keeps its word, and you will have to be told it. Only this: under the worn part there was a second line, and it did not wear away. Somebody cut it out, stroke by stroke, with a patient knife."
- PROPOSED: "Nothing comes up that you can read. The stone keeps its word, and you will have to hear it from someone else. Only this much is plain: under the worn part there was a second line, and it did not wear away. Somebody cut it out, stroke by stroke, with a patient knife."
- WHY: "you will have to be told it" is stiff. "Hear it from someone else" also points the player at Garrick's line that follows. The pun on "keeps its word" stays.

### W5D2 · The old kingdom's road

**2. The block appears (ep5 line 167)**
- CURRENT: "At the rows' end, the block's top lifts off like a lid."
- PROPOSED: "At the far end of the rows stands one squared block of stone that is no grave. Its top lifts off like a lid."
- WHY: only Craft players are told about "the block" (in the notice line). Everyone else meets "the block's" with no idea what it is. The Craft line keeps its own insight ("cut like the waystone at Reedham").

**3. The Pull (ep5 line 206)**
- CURRENT: "**It is nearer every dusk, and it is coming from inside the city. Highwall, by noon.**"
- PROPOSED: "**The heartbeat is nearer every dusk, and it is coming from inside the city. Highwall, by noon.**"
- WHY: the last thing on screen was the family and the child, so "It" could mean anything. The Pull should be unmistakable.

**4. Small fix (ep5 line 155)**
- CURRENT: "the ones who fell in the war, before the last wall."
- PROPOSED: "the ones who fell in the war, before the last night at the wall."
- WHY: "before the last wall" reads like a place name. The new wording ties to the Seekers' night on the wall, which the player reaches tomorrow. Optional.

### W5D3 · The herald's gate

**5. Who "they" are (ep5 line 240)**
- CURRENT: "This is the wall they held that night."
- PROPOSED: "This is the wall the Seekers held that night."
- WHY: the line straight above is scripture about "*Their* herald" (the other side). The next "they" then means the opposite group, and players will stumble. Naming the Seekers once also makes line 290 ("where they would have stood shoulder to shoulder") and line 298 ("You are standing where they stood") read clearly. The scripture line itself is untouched.

### W5D4 · The dead streets

No changes. The soldiers' street, the press detail (all stopped on the same step), the turned helm and the watch with Garrick are already excellent.

### W5D5 · Below the hall

No changes. The overheard greeting, the board and "They are not laying siege to him. They are keeping count." all work. This is a model day.

### W5D6 · The wall again

**6. The lookout (ep5 line 533)**
- CURRENT: "Mei goes to the corner of the gate-tower, where Mei can see the square and you can see Mei's hand."
- PROPOSED: "Mei crouches at the corner of the gate-tower, with the whole square in view, and one hand raised where you can see it."
- WHY: "Mei" three times in one sentence (to avoid a pronoun) is clumsy. This names the friend once and needs no pronoun.

**7. Pass branch (ep5 line 560): a rule problem, see (a)**
- CURRENT: "Her hand drops. You cross. Nothing in the square so much as turns."
- PROPOSED: "The hand drops. You cross, forty paces of open stone, and nothing in the square so much as turns."
- WHY: "Her" assumes the posted friend is a woman, but the player may post Dev or Jo. It also gives the rushed one-liner a little weight.

## Week 6

### W6D1 · Six nights

**8. Before the Challenge (ep6 line 66)**
- CURRENT: "They lived here. They would have left the start of their way here, too."
- PROPOSED: "They lived in this room. If the Seekers left a way into his hall, the first step of it would be here."
- WHY: "the start of their way" is vague. The player needs to know what they are searching the room for. It matches the Pull ("the Seekers left a way in, somewhere").

**9. Pass branch (ep6 line 78)**
- CURRENT: "You find it before the light has moved."
- PROPOSED: "You find it before the light at the slit has moved, with the stair below still quiet."
- WHY: the fail branch is four tense lines and the pass is six words. One clause of place lets the pass feel like a win, and it reveals nothing about the find.

**10. The watch-page (ep6 line 95)**
- CURRENT: "You are holding the watch-page that came off his dead brother. It still shows seven, and nobody is left to mark it."
- PROPOSED: "You are holding the watch-page you took from the dead watchman's fist. It still shows seven, and nobody is left to mark it."
- WHY: "his dead brother" is unclear. It could mean the watchman at the board, or a real brother, which would be a new fact. Gathering 5 says the page came "from the watchman's fist", so this is exact.

### W6D2 · Five nights

**11. Opening (ep6 lines 142–144)**
- CURRENT: "You go out by the gate-tower, and past the crossroads where the rest of the soldiers stood. / The street is empty."
- PROPOSED: "You go out by the gate-tower in the grey morning, and down to the crossroads where the rest of the soldiers stood, mid-stride, for longer than anyone has been alive. / The street is empty."
- WHY: this is a big turn (the dead have moved), but it opens on a bare travel line. Recalling what used to stand there makes "The street is empty." land. No new facts.

**12. Repeated phrase (ep6 line 178)**
- CURRENT: "Nobody breathes."
- PROPOSED: "Nobody makes a sound."
- WHY: W6D1 opened on the same "Nobody breathes." the day before. Optional.

**13. "The others" (ep6 line 198)**
- CURRENT: "On the way back you find the others: three streets further up the hill than they were, as still as before."
- PROPOSED: "On the way back you pass the rest of the soldiers: three streets further up the hill than they were, as still as before."
- WHY: "the others" first reads as the player's own friends, which is a real misreading in a party game.

### W6D3 · Four nights

**14. Opening, time order (ep6 lines 234–238)**
- CURRENT: "Tonight the heartbeat shakes the dust off the rooftops in thin grey sheets. / `[STRIKE THE PAGE]` / At dusk, in the wall room. Four."
- PROPOSED: "At dusk, in the wall room, the heartbeat shakes the dust off the rooftops outside in thin grey sheets. The page is waiting. / `[STRIKE THE PAGE]` / Four."
- WHY: as written, "Tonight" comes first, then the stroke, then "At dusk", so the player meets the time back to front with no scene set. This puts the place and time first and keeps the stroke as the act. (The file also has a stray empty `>` line before the button at line 235.)

**15. Aim notice has no second sentence (ep6 line 260)**
- CURRENT: "**Aim** · A watchman walks the square with a lantern, and turns at the same flagstone every time."
- PROPOSED: "**Aim** · A watchman walks the square with a lantern, and turns at the same flagstone every time. *Past that stone, he never looks.*"
- WHY: every other notice line has an italic second sentence for Adept players. Without one, an Adept Aim player gets nothing extra. It only sharpens what the line already says.

### W6D4 · Three nights

**16. Into the crack (ep6 line 372)**
- CURRENT: "You go in sideways. Deep inside, where no light reaches without a lamp, a housing is set into the stone."
- PROPOSED: "You go in sideways, one at a time, with the lamp held out ahead. Deep inside, where the last of the grey daylight gives out, a housing is set into the stone."
- WHY: "where no light reaches without a lamp" is circular and flat. This gives the squeeze its feel. The Agility notice already says it is single file.

### W6D5 · Two nights

No changes. Counting forward with a fingertip to the doorpost is the best beat of the week.

### W6D6 · One night

**17. Aim notice has no second sentence (ep6 line 547)**
- CURRENT: "**Aim** · At the top of the street, where the hill begins, one of them has stopped."
- PROPOSED: "**Aim** · At the top of the street, where the hill begins, one of them has stopped. *It is not facing the hall.*"
- WHY: the same gap as item 15. It foreshadows line 566 ("turned round") without giving more away.

## (a) Lines that break a rule as written
1. **ep5 line 560, W5D6 pass: "Her hand drops."** The lookout is whichever friend the player posts (`[POST MEI]` `[POST DEV]` `[POST JO]`), so "Her" misgenders any man in the party. Fixed in item 7. Also check the generated `src/data/chapterScripts.ts` and the online `chapters` table, which carry this text.
2. No reassurance before risk, no out-of-game talk, no gate broken (Highwall first appears on W5D1, "the Order" and "five" are well past their gates), and the glossary words are right. Nothing else breaks a rule.

## (b) The changes I'd prioritise
1. **Item 7** ("Her hand drops"): a rule fix, and the lookout line (item 6) goes with it.
2. **Item 13** ("the others" → "the rest of the soldiers"): a real misreading risk at a tense moment.
3. **Item 5** ("the wall the Seekers held"): clears up who is who on the day the Seekers' trail begins.
4. **Item 2** (introduce the block on W5D2): most players never see the Craft line, so "the block" comes from nowhere.
5. **Item 14** (W6D3 opening order), with items 15 and 17 (the missing Adept sentences).
