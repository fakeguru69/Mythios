# Prose audit: Weeks 3 and 4, weekdays (Days 1 to 6)

**Files:** `docs/canon/episode-3-the-fire-stone.md` (lines 32–631) and `docs/canon/episode-4-the-water-stone.md` (lines 32–615). Day 7 is not covered here.

**What I checked:** 12 weekday chapters, about 190 quoted passages. That covers the narration, every "What you notice" line, the Challenge and fight cards, the pass and fail branches, the [PRESS ON] details, the Pulls, the day summary cards, and the lines spoken by Corin, Wren's mother, the boy at the well, the carter, the fisherman and Joss's grandmother. I also checked the first-use gates, the gold on the cards, and whether the writers' notes in the text reach players (they do not: `scripts/gen-chapter-scripts.cjs` line 101 strips them).

**Overall verdict:** these two weeks are among the best-written in the season. The prose is concrete and quiet. Endings like "Not blown out. Gone in." and "Until today, these people have never once hurried." land well, and the clues are always clear. The weak spots are small and fall into four groups:
1. **Thin pass branches.** Several are one functional line ("The way opens cleanly." "You reach the tower in good order.") and read like a report, not a scene.
2. **Two fragment finds lose the "cold" beat.** Week 4 Days 4 and 5 drop the per-place cold that every other find has.
3. **Two openings are one or two lines.** Week 3 Day 6 and Week 4 Day 6 open the last weekday of their week with almost nothing.
4. **A few clarity snags.** A jump in location, an ambiguous fork, an unclear notice line, and one "Either way" that shows the game's branching.

Nothing below changes a fact, clue, number, outcome or Garrick line. I did not touch any [PLANT] line.

## Week 3 (The Fire Stone)

### Day 1. Somewhere warm

**1. Jump from the old room to the shaft mouth** (line 65)
- CURRENT: "You are at the mouth of the old shaft, in the grey before dawn, when something comes up out of the hill behind you."
- PROPOSED: "By the time you have brought Corin up the old shaft, the sky is going grey. You are standing at its mouth, with the boy half asleep against somebody's shoulder, when something comes up out of the hill behind you."
- WHY: the last scene was in lamplight underground. As written, the player is suddenly outside with no sense of how they got there, and Corin is missing from the frame.

**2. "Does not say" twice** (fight card, line 71, then line 76)
- CURRENT: "He does not say what he wants." (card), followed by "He does not say a word." (aftermath)
- PROPOSED card line: "He has come up alone, and he has not come to talk."
- WHY: the card and the aftermath repeat the same idea one beat apart. The new card line keeps the menace and leaves "He does not say a word" its full weight.

**3. Summary card** (line 120): good. Optional cut: "The ledger, Meira's list and the cart tracks all say so." could become "The ledger, Meira's list and the cart tracks all point there." This is minor: "say so" reads a little like a checklist.

### Day 2. Ashcombe

**4. Pass branch** (line 164)
- CURRENT: "The door opens. She stands back to let you in."
- PROPOSED: "A bolt draws back. The door opens a hand's width, then wider, and she looks at each of you for a long moment before she stands back to let you in."
- WHY: this is the payoff of the whole Challenge, and two flat sentences undersell it. The Charm notice line ("She wanted a longer look at you") already sets up the long look.

**5. The boy at the well: who is telling this?** (line 201)
- CURRENT: "He was up after dark last night, where he should not have been. He saw a cart come through the village without lamps. …"
- PROPOSED: "In a whisper, with one eye on the doors, he tells you what he saw. He was up after dark last night, where he should not have been. A cart came through the village without lamps. …" (the rest unchanged)
- WHY: as written, the narrator suddenly knows the boy's night. A short lead-in makes it plain that this is his account, and still gives him no spoken lines (the designer's note asks for that).

The rest of Day 2 is strong and should stay as written: the mother's three answers, the dust-free chips on the sill, and "If Wren was in that cart…".

### Day 3. The Blackglass

**6. Pass branch** (line 269)
- CURRENT: "You reach the far side clean."
- PROPOSED: "You reach the far side clean, without once putting a hand to the glass."
- WHY: this is a small, physical reward for the pass. It is the opposite of the fail branch's slide.

**7. "Either way" shows the game's branching** (line 274)
- CURRENT: "Either way, the far edge takes its toll. Where the glass has cracked its edges are as sharp as knives, and you come off it with one boot split open along the sole."
- PROPOSED: "The far edge takes its toll all the same. Where the glass has cracked, its edges are as sharp as knives, and you come off it with one boot split open along the sole."
- WHY: "Either way" is the writer speaking about the pass and fail branches, not the storyteller speaking to the player. I also added the missing comma after "cracked", which makes the sentence easier to read aloud.

### Day 4. The black ridges

**8. Aim notice line has no second sentence** (line 366)
- CURRENT: "**Aim** · A hundred yards off the riders' line there is a shadow that is too straight."
- PROPOSED: "**Aim** · A hundred yards off the riders' line there is a shadow that is too straight. *The riders had their eyes on the trail. You did not.*"
- WHY: every other notice line has a second sentence for Adept and above. An Aim-led Adept player gets nothing extra here. (The same gap is in Day 6; see item 12.)

**9. Pass branch** (line 389)
- CURRENT: "The way opens cleanly."
- PROPOSED: "Stone by stone the fall comes away, and what is left above it holds. The way opens cleanly."
- WHY: the card set up a tense, stone-by-stone job ("One wrong stone…"). The pass should let the player feel it hold.

**10. "Whoever" three times in a row** (lines 396–400)
- CURRENT: "Whoever wrote your fragments slept here… / In a crack in the nearest niche is a single die… / Whoever slept here had long nights too."
- PROPOSED last line: "They had long nights here too."
- WHY: two "Whoever" sentences two lines apart read as an echo by accident. "They" is clear because the fragment-writers were named two lines earlier, and it matches the summary card's wording ("They had long nights too").

### Day 5. The shepherd's hut

**11. The grey coat's entrance** (line 458)
- CURRENT: "A grey coat stands up on the ridge above the hut. He was left behind to watch. He comes down."
- PROPOSED: "On the ridge above the hut a grey coat stands up from among the rocks, where he was left behind to watch. He comes down to meet you."
- WHY: three short sentences in a row read like stage directions. One flowing line plus a short closing beat keeps the threat and reads better aloud.

The rest of Day 5 is excellent and needs no change: the hut search, "Until today, these people have never once hurried.", and "They saw your fire."

### Day 6. The cairn

**12. Opening is one line** (line 548)
- CURRENT: "The cairn stands on the highest ridge in the burned country, and the light is going."
- PROPOSED: "You see the cairn from a long way off: a dark thumb of stones on the highest ridge in the burned country, against a sky already turning pale. Between you and it lies ridge after ridge of black rock. And the light is going."
- WHY: this is the last weekday of the week, and it opens on a single line straight into the notice line. The new text sets the distance and the race against the light that the Challenge card ("an hour of light, and more than an hour of ridge") then pays off. It adds no new facts.

**13. Aim notice line has no second sentence** (line 556)
- CURRENT: "**Aim** · Over one hill, and only one, the air is moving like the air over a forge."
- PROPOSED: add "*Something under that hill is still hot.*"
- WHY: this has the same Adept gap as item 8. The new sentence repeats what the pass branch later shows ("as if the hill were breathing") and adds nothing new.

Keep as written: the map words "In through the crack. Down a way cut long ago. To a room beneath the hill." (they are said aloud, a [PLANT]), and the lamp that "goes in".

## Week 4 (The Water Stone)

### Day 1. Not finished

**14. Aim notice line is unclear** (line 49)
- CURRENT: "**Aim** · The ash is smooth right across the floor, but for your own prints and one line of his. *For the moment.*"
- PROPOSED: "… one line of his. *It will not stay that way for long.*"
- WHY: "For the moment" is cryptic on its own. The new line says the same thing plainly: the next careless boot will spoil it. This also sets up the Challenge.

**15. The walk back is clumsy** (line 98)
- CURRENT: "You bring Wren up out of the Kiln into a grey dawn. Straight back across the ridges it is one long day's walk; the trail she left you wandered far more."
- PROPOSED: "You bring Wren up out of the Kiln into a grey dawn. Straight back across the ridges, Ashcombe is one long day's walk. The trail of glass she left you wandered for days."
- WHY: "wandered far more" is vague, and it is not clear where the walk ends. Naming the destination and the "trail of glass" ties it back to Week 3.

**16. Optional: "sleeve" again.** Corin "has not let go of somebody's sleeve" (Week 3 Day 1). Wren "has not let go of the sleeve" and walks "with her hand in somebody's sleeve" (Week 4 Day 1, lines 47 and 61). This is three sleeves across two chapters. If the echo is not deliberate, line 61 could read "she walks, holding tight to somebody's hand." This is low priority; it may be a deliberate rhyme between the rescued children.

### Day 2. The north road

**17. The fork: which road is whose?** (lines 173–179)
- CURRENT: "Beyond the ford the road forks. Mei and Jo walk the left. You take the right. … The lake road, the one to Reedham, has nothing on it but sheep. The other has four horses…"
- PROPOSED: "Beyond the ford the road forks. Mei and Jo take the lake road, the one to Reedham. You take the other." Then after [READ THE GROUND]: "Mei and Jo's road has nothing on it but sheep. Yours has four horses and two thin wheels, a day old, going fast."
- WHY: "left" and "right" are never tied to "the lake road" and "the other". The player cannot tell whether they found the tracks or their friends did. The facts are unchanged.

**18. Pass branch** (line 200)
- CURRENT: "He gets to the point."
- PROPOSED: "You steer him off the weather in three questions, and he gets to the point."
- WHY: the fail branch is funny and full ("his brother-in-law"). The pass should show the skill that earned it, and keep the same light touch.

### Day 3. Reedham

**19. The doorpost and the notches are now one beat** (lines 264–268)
- CURRENT: "After that she only stands, with her hand on the doorpost." [new block] "Notches, cut with a kitchen knife, fresh and pale in the old wood. One for every night. / Eight."
- PROPOSED: "After that she only stands, with her hand on the doorpost. Under her fingers are notches, cut with a kitchen knife, fresh and pale in the old wood. One for every night. / Eight."
- WHY: since the 15 Oct change, the doorpost is no longer a choice. Without the old button, the two blocks now read as a jump. "Under her fingers" links what she is touching to what she is counting, which is the moment of the day.

**20. Pass branch** (line 307)
- CURRENT: "You reach the tower in good order."
- PROPOSED: "You bring her in under the tower with the bilge still shallow and the wind rising at your back."
- WHY: "in good order" is report language. The new line fits both the Craft and Might options and keeps the lake and the wind in view.

### Day 4. Old Reedham

**21. The fragment find has no cold** (line 401)
- CURRENT: "The stone lid slides back under your fingers."
- PROPOSED: "The stone lid slides back under your fingers. The silt round it is warm from the sun; what lies inside is as cold as the deep water further out."
- WHY: every other find in both weeks tells its cold through its place (the 29 Sep series audit asked for this). This one, and Day 5's, were left without it.

**22. Heading and summary title differ (small).** The heading is "The drowned streets" (line 350, and so `chapterScripts.ts` line 3398). The section title and summary card say "OLD REEDHAM". Every other day uses one name for both. Suggest the heading "Old Reedham." to match, unless the difference is deliberate.

### Day 5. The reed island

**23. The fragment find has no cold** (line 506)
- CURRENT: "The moment they touch the lid, it opens."
- PROPOSED: "The moment they touch the lid, it opens, and the cold that comes out of it is the cold of the lake bed."
- WHY: this has the same gap as item 21. "Lake bed" suits a housing half swallowed by willow roots at the waterline.

### Day 6. The tower at night

**24. Opening is two short lines** (line 542)
- CURRENT: "The lamp is out long before you reach the tower. There is nobody in it."
- PROPOSED: "You row out after it in their own boat, with her skiff on its line behind, and the oars as quiet as you can make them. The lamp is out long before you reach the tower. When you climb in, there is nobody there."
- WHY: this follows straight on from yesterday's Pull ("After that lamp, tonight, in their own boat"). The new opening pays off that Pull and puts the player in the boat, on the black water, before the twist. It sets up the "Intellect" notice line ("no boat passed you going back"). It adds no new facts.

**25. Optional: two "last fragment" lines that sound alike.** Week 3 Day 6 has "not hidden, only wedged between the lowest stones". Week 4 Day 6 has "not hidden at all: wedged in the angle of a step". If the echo is not deliberate, Week 4's could begin "The last fragment is waiting on the stair, in plain sight: wedged in the angle of a step…". This is low priority.

## (a) Lines that break a rule as written

- **None found.** The first-use gates hold: "the Order" (Week 3 Day 5), "five" (Meira's footnote, Week 3 Day 3), "king" (Week 4 Day 2 coin) and "moon-blood" (Week 4 Day 4) are all on or after their gate days. "heartbeat" and "Highwall" do not appear.
- Fight pay matches the rules: 300g on Week 3 Days 1 and 5 (no Challenge that day), and no reward line on Week 4 Days 2 and 5 (both have a Challenge).
- There is no reassurance before a risk and no out-of-game talk.
- The two writers' notes inside quote blocks (Week 3 line 400 "*(Moved 15 Oct…)*" and Week 4 line 264 "*(Changed 15 Oct…)*") are stripped by the generator and never reach players. I checked `chapterScripts.ts`.
- Format gaps (not rule breaks): two notice lines have no Adept second sentence (items 8 and 13), and one heading does not match its summary title (item 22).
- Week 4 Day 1 Garrick line ("not one of you has died"): this is a Garrick line and a [PLANT], so it is out of scope. It comes after the danger, not before a risk, so it does not break the reassurance rule.

## (b) Changes to make first

1. **Week 4 Day 6 opening** (item 24) and **Week 3 Day 6 opening** (item 12). The last weekday of each week opens with almost nothing. These are the clearest cases of "a one or two liner".
2. **Week 4 Day 2 fork clarity** (item 17). As written, a player cannot tell who found the riders' tracks.
3. **Week 4 Day 3 doorpost to notches** (item 19). Since the 15 Oct change the day's key moment reads as a jump, and three words fix it.
4. **The thin pass branches** (items 4, 6, 9, 18, 20), done as one pass. A passed Challenge should feel earned, not reported.
5. **The missing fragment cold** (items 21 and 23), and the "Either way" line (item 7).
