# Prose audit: the six Gatherings and the United Effort stories

**What I checked.** The Day 7 section of all six episode files (`docs/canon/episode-1-the-list.md` line 718 to `episode-6-the-finale.md` line 976): Arrival, the Approach (card, pass and fail lines), Battle narration, Notice and [LOOK CLOSER], each United Effort's scene and endings, the five Verses, the torn page, the Watch, the untorn page, Match quote-backs, the Ask boards, the turns, the Cliffhangers and the summary cards. Also all of `docs/canon/mythios-united-effort-stories.md` (six scenes, 84 band lines, hand-ons, between-turn lines, endings). About 230 passages read; I propose changes to 31 of them.

**Overall verdict.** This is the strongest writing in the canon. The scripture (the Verse of the Answering above all, then the Fall, the Four, the Binding, the Watch and the Name) already sounds old, beautiful and ominous, and it stays clear. I would leave every Verse almost exactly as it is. Most of the United Effort lines are vivid and concrete ("a beam you would not have trusted with a cat"; "the hill shuts like a book"). The problems are mostly at the edges:
- a few **one-line beats at big moments** (the finale's United Effort endings; Gathering 3's Approach has no pass or fail line at all);
- **repeated phrases** (Gathering 5 says "every night of his life" three times and prints one sentence twice; two Battles open with the same "[N] of them, between you and…" pattern);
- **stage-direction brackets** shown to players inside the scripture;
- **gendered hand-on lines** in the United Effort file (they say "she" and "her" for whichever friend is named);
- **the United Effort file and the episode files disagree** in five places.

Nothing below changes a plot fact, a clue, a number, a reward or an outcome. No [PLANT] line, Garrick line, Match quote or older-hand line is touched.

**Words that must not move (scripture and clue lines).** I propose framing changes only around these. Their wording stays exactly as written:
- G2: *"Blood beneath the dark moon — and it answers."*; *"…one wall, and one king upon it."*; *"He opened his palm with his own knife."*; *"give the marked blood in his name"*; *Until the throne rise whole.*; *given.*
- G3: *"They no longer felt the cold, nor the blade, nor the long fear of dying."*; *"He never spoke a false word to his people — not one. He only kept silent about the price."*; *"set their own blood as the lock"*; *"the faithful shall not lie… Hide inside the truth."*
- G4: *"Their blood remembers what the shards remember."*; *"Where the Seekers' children walk, the faithful follow"*; *"until the sky itself remembers the night he answered"*; the whole torn page, word for word, and "the older hand".
- G5: *"The fifth drinks deep of moon-blood, and of no other."*; *"Those who had stood nearest him, when he fell, stood on."*; *"They kept it near him, under their own eyes."*; *Count the nights.*; the tally of seven.
- G6: *"They had known him all his life as one of the marked."*; *"Moon-blood. It was a title…"*; *"We were only servants, they said."*; the untorn page, word for word.

## Gathering 1. The mine (`episode-1-the-list.md`)

### 1.1 Arrival (lines 726–730): thin for the first live night
CURRENT:
> Evening finds you where the map said you would be: an old mine cut into the far riverbank, its mouth half drowned by the river.
>
> There is torchlight just inside. There should not be.
>
> Garrick knows a way in. He has known it since he was a boy, he says.

PROPOSED:
> Evening finds you where the map said you would be. Across the ford, the far bank rises in a wall of wet black rock, and cut into it is an old mine, its mouth half drowned by the river. Rotten pit-props lean in the entrance like broken teeth. Nobody has worked this ground in fifty years.
>
> There is torchlight just inside. There should not be.
>
> Garrick knows a way in. He has known it since he was a boy, he says.

WHY: this is the party's first time together, live. One beat of place sets the scene before the vote. "Fifty years" is already in the Approach, and the last two lines are untouched. Garrick's reported speech is unchanged.

### 1.2 "It is over." (line 769): abrupt
CURRENT: `> It is over.`
PROPOSED: `> It is over. A dropped torch is still rolling on the boards, and then there is no sound in the mine but the river.`
WHY: the first Battle deserves a breath of aftermath before Tamsin. The river also sets up the drop.

### 1.3 Notice, From the floor (line 787): "daylight" at night
CURRENT: `> **From the floor** · From down here you can see daylight between the gallery's floorboards, and it is not daylight: it is the river, a long way below.`
PROPOSED: `> **From the floor** · From down here you can see light between the gallery's floorboards, and it is not torchlight: it is the river, a long way below, catching the torches.`
WHY: it is evening in a mine, so "daylight" puzzles the reader. The clue (the river far below the floor) is kept.

## Gathering 2. Under the mill hill (`episode-2-the-wind-stone.md`)

### 2.1 The black circle (lines 710–716): repeats the Aim notice, and the last line is a trailer
For an Aim friend, line 710 repeats their notice line (696) almost word for word ("a small book bound in black cloth… half trodden underfoot… a lamp").

CURRENT:
> Beside the table, dropped in the fight and half trodden underfoot, lies a small book bound in black cloth, one corner scorched where it fell against a lamp.

PROPOSED:
> Somebody lifts the book out from under the fallen lamp and beats the sparks out of its corner. It is small, bound in black cloth, and warm from the flame.

CURRENT (line 716):
> The kidnappers have a name. And the Order's scripture is about to tell you who they pray to.

PROPOSED:
> The kidnappers have a name. And somewhere in these pages is the one they pray to.

WHY: the first change avoids repeating the notice. The second turns a narrator's trailer ("is about to tell you") into something the party feels in the room. "The Order" stays as written; it is story text at Gathering 2.

### 2.2 The older hand (line 790): a stage direction shown to players inside the Verse
The generated script shows this bracket to players in bold (`gatheringScripts.ts` line 588).

CURRENT:
> > **[Set apart on the page, in an older hand — words older than the Order's own name, and nothing like the plain, careful hand the Seekers left in every fragment]**

PROPOSED (as a narrated line in the same italic as the Verse, not a bracket):
> > *Here the page changes. One line is set apart, in an older hand: words older than the Order's own name, and nothing like the plain, careful hand the Seekers left in every fragment.*

WHY: it reads as part of the book, not as a note to the builder. "Older hand", "older than the Order's own name" and the Seekers' "plain, careful hand" are all kept, because Gathering 4 refers back to them. **The quoted line itself does not move.** The same treatment fits G4's `[Set apart, in the older hand]` and `[The Order's own hand]`, G5's `[Set apart, in the older hand]` and G6's `[The older hand, untorn]` (see 4.5, 5.5, 6.6).

## Gathering 3. Under the Kiln (`episode-3-the-fire-stone.md`)

### 3.1 The Approach has no pass or fail line (after line 667)
Every other Gathering tells the volunteer what their roll did. Here the story goes straight to the drop.

PROPOSED, *On a pass:*
> It holds. One by one you come down into the warm dark without a sound, and nobody below looks up.

PROPOSED, *On a failure:*
> The black glass goes out from under you in a long, ringing rush, and you arrive at the bottom in a heap, in a noise like a dresser of plates going over.

WHY: it fills a gap the other five nights don't have. Both lines only describe what the card already says (a quiet arrival, or "a slide in a rattle of black glass").

### 3.2 "for one heartbeat" (line 669)
CURRENT: `…and for one heartbeat you see him:`
PROPOSED: `…and for one moment you see him:`
WHY: from Week 4 Day 6 the season's great sound is "like a heartbeat". This keeps the word fresh for it. The gate binds the build's own text, not episode prose, so this is a suggestion and not a rule break.

### 3.3 Battle opening (line 677): thin
CURRENT:
> They are waiting for you. [N] of them, a blade in one hand and a torch in the other. They do not break when the first of them falls.

PROPOSED:
> They are waiting for you. [N] of them, robed, a blade in one hand and a torch in the other, and the heat of the Kiln pressing down on all of you like a hand. Nobody speaks. They do not break when the first of them falls.

WHY: it adds the place (the Kiln's heat) and gives the fight weight. No fact changes.

### 3.4 After the Battle (line 687): out-of-game talk in the narration
CURRENT: `> He lets himself be bound. There is no fight left in it, and no roll.`
PROPOSED: `> He lets himself be bound. There is no fight left in him, and none is asked of you.`
WHY: "no roll" is game talk inside the story (CLAUDE.md §1 rule 10). It is listed under (a) as well.

### 3.5 The Verse of the Fall (line 787): the same verb twice
CURRENT: `*There is a price for every gift, and he never spoke of this one. He never spoke a false word to his people — not one. He only kept silent about the price.*`
PROPOSED: `*There is a price for every gift, and this one he never named. He never spoke a false word to his people — not one. He only kept silent about the price.*`
WHY: "never spoke… never spoke" sounds like a slip, not a deliberate echo. **The second and third sentences are the Match quote and do not move.**

### 3.6 Notice and Ask: very good, leave. The Cliffhanger: very good, leave.

## Gathering 5. The wall at Highwall (`episode-5-the-kings-stone.md`)

### 5.1 "every night of his life" three times, and one sentence printed twice
- Line 678 (end of Battle): *"…the words of a page he must have said every night of his life."*
- Line 690 (United Effort): *"…as he has answered it every night of his life."*
- Line 707 (Roll-Call reaches 0): the whole of line 678 again, word for word.

PROPOSED, line 678:
> Kneel close enough and you can hear it. He's praying — the words of a page worn soft with saying.

PROPOSED, line 707 (the Roll-Call reaches 0 ending):
> There is time, now.
>
> You kneel beside him in the street. His eyes have not left the hall, and his lips have not stopped moving. Now it is quiet enough to hear the words.

WHY: line 690 keeps "every night of his life", where it lands hardest. The ending no longer repeats the Battle's sentence. The same fix is needed in `mythios-united-effort-stories.md` line 518.

### 5.2 Arrival (line 634): "up the hill" and "on its hill" are hard to picture
CURRENT: `> Night in Highwall. No lamp burns anywhere in the dead city but the Order's fire up the hill, and there's no sound at all but your own breath.`
PROPOSED: `> Night in Highwall. No lamp burns anywhere in the dead city but one: the Order's fire, high on the hill below his hall. There's no sound at all but your own breath.`
WHY: later lines (the watchmen come "down the hill"; the square "below the hall") place the fire on the hall's own hill. Saying so once at the start makes the whole night easier to picture.

### 5.3 The hidden room (line 775): a flat word
CURRENT: `It's the first Stone any of you has ever actually held.`
PROPOSED: `It is the first Stone any of you has ever held in your own hands.`
WHY: "actually" is spoken filler. The fact is unchanged.

### 5.4 Cliffhanger tally (lines 854–864): the count reads as if it should drop to six
The page says "Seven", then a watchman strikes "tonight's stroke, late" and the text says "Seven left". A reader expects six. Canon says 7 at the end of Gathering 5, so the number is right; only the reading is unclear.

CURRENT: `…takes up the chalk and draws it through tonight's stroke, late. Seven left.`
PROPOSED: `…takes up the chalk and draws it through tonight's stroke, late. Now the board and the page agree: seven left.`
WHY: it makes the count clear without changing it: the page had already counted tonight. Please check this against the Week 5 Day 5 count (8) before applying.

### 5.5 The older-hand bracket (line 736): treat as in 2.2.

### 5.6 The Verse of the Binding and the Watch: superb ("as a sleeper's hand opens"; "let no night pass uncounted"). Leave them.

## (a) Lines that break a rule as written

1. **Out-of-game talk:** G3, `episode-3-the-fire-stone.md` line 687, "There is no fight left in it, and no roll." Fix in 3.4.
2. **Gendered hand-on lines** for real friends: United Effort file, lines 286, 396, 398 and 588. Fix in UE.1.
3. **Number labels:** the Hawser's band labels (United Effort file lines 566–584: "40 to 59", "20 to 39", "1 to 19") are still on the old 60 scale. Since 5 Oct the job starts at 100 (Part 7, Part 9 item 1). The build uses bands, not these numbers, so play is not affected, but the document is wrong. They should read 67 to 99, 34 to 66, 1 to 33 (60 only for a friend alone).
4. **The rule of three between-turn lines** is not met by the Hawser (UE.4).
5. **The United Effort file disagrees with the episode files** in five places (UE.2). The "two nights" against "a night and a day" mismatch contradicts a Notice clue.
6. **A small story contradiction:** G4's Might notice says the Kiln's faithful "stood until the last of them fell". The last one surrendered (4.3).
7. *(Not a break, for the record:)* G1 line 789, "It is a roll, and a miss costs nothing", is a true teaching line about [LOOK CLOSER], not reassurance before a risk. I left it.

## (b) The changes I would do first

1. **Gathering 5's repeats (5.1):** one sentence printed twice and "every night of his life" three times, in the season's most solemn scene. The players will notice.
2. **The finale's United Effort endings (6.1) and the missing [DO NOTHING] line (6.3):** the last shared win, and the season's last choice, should not end on one line or on nothing.
3. **The scripture brackets turned into narrated framing (2.2, 4.5, 5.5, 6.6):** at the moment players read holy writing, a bold stage direction breaks the spell. The clue words stay.
4. **Gathering 3's missing Approach lines and "no roll" (3.1, 3.4):** a gap in the pattern, and the one rule break in the night text.
5. **The United Effort file fixes (UE.1, UE.2):** the pronouns and the five mismatches, before any builder copies from that file.
